## Autarkia 0.11.1 (first public alpha)

First public release. It contains all six tiers. License: MIT, so add-ons, bridges and compatibility mods are welcome.

- **Tier 1 Muscle and Timber:** hand power, rope drives, windmills, flywheels, the Colony Core and Porters.
- **Tier 2 Steam and Copper:** boilers, steam engines, copper wire with heat and sag, lightning, belts, racks, the Pattern Loom, Hauler Crabs.
- **Tier 3 Electric Works:** turbines, ore processing, capsule tubes, the warehouse, Builder Drones, Loaders, Linemen, failure memory.
- **Tier 4 Frontier:** outposts, high-voltage lines, hydro and wind power, cable cars, crate launchers, Surveyors, Cable-Layers, Sentinels.
- **Tier 5 Matter:** lattice crystals, plasma power, superconductors with quench, the Matter Buffer, mag rails, nanite claims, the Replicator Walker.
- **Tier 6 Ascendant:** the Singularity Core, phase links, the Matter Compiler, the Autarky Seed, the Restoration Field, Bastion blocks, the Forge of Ascension and Aegis gear.
- **Integrations:** JEI recipe categories and info pages, Jade tooltips, an optional YACL config screen, and the GuideME Field Manual with a chapter and a 3D scene per tier.
- **Command:** `/autarkia profile` shows the per-tick server cost of the Autarkia systems.

**Known limits:** Tier 6 and the newest integrations are not yet tested in game. Back up your world before you use them.
