## Autarkia 0.15.18

- **Dormant colonies can be recovered.** If you lose the linked seed, craft a new Core Seed and plant it in the same dimension. It brings back your dormant colony.\n- **Wrench:** it no longer picks up a Colony Core, because that gave no seed.\n- **New admin command:** /autarkia colony dissolve <name> deletes a colony. Its blocks and workers stay in the world.
