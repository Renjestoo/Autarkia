## Autarkia 0.14.5

- **Big machines follow their model.** The 38 machines with a 3D model and a footprint of more than one block no longer use a full box for every block of the footprint. The game builds the shape of each machine from its model on a 4-pixel grid. The selection outline now follows the model, you can reach through the empty space around it (for example past the side of the Colony Core), and you walk only into the parts that are really there.
- Examples: the Colony Core (3 x 3 x 3), the windmill tower, the waterwheel, the poles and pylons, and the open chamber of the Forge of Ascension.
