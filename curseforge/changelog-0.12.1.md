## Autarkia 0.12.1

- **Cable terminals on every machine:** each machine, generator and storage block now has a visible cable fitting: a wooden rope sheave on tier 1 blocks, a ceramic bushing with a copper cap from tier 2. Big machines have 2 to 4 fittings, and a cable leaves from the fitting that faces its other end. Before, cables ended at the middle of a block face, floated above big machines, or ran back into the machine.
- **Connectors:** ropes leave the rope pulley at the rim, cables end in the wall insulator cap, and cryo node cables end on 4 couplers at the head rim. The transformer and fuse box have 2 bushings each.
- Spans strung before 0.12.1 keep their old ends until you string them again.
