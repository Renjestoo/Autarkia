## Autarkia 0.12.0

- **Getting Started tab:** a new advancement tab leads you step by step, from the first tin ore to a tier 6 colony. Each step says what to do next.
- **Field Manual at the start:** every player gets one Field Manual on the first join of a world (config `colony.starterManual`). Before, it came only after the Colony Core grew.
- **Field Manual fixes:** 3D scenes of big machines are no longer cut off; a broken recipe box (Electric Drill) and a broken item link (Brass Ingot) are fixed.
- **Power lines:** cables now end on the insulators of wooden poles, steel pylons and HV pylons, and on top of big machines instead of inside them. Metal cables take the world light (they no longer glow in the dark) and no longer draw through themselves. Spans strung before 0.12.0 keep their old ends until you string them again.
