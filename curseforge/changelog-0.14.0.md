## Autarkia 0.14.0

- **12 machines remodeled with moving parts:** plate press (ram presses), wire drawer (capstan and spool turn), magnetic separator (drum turns, belt runs), electrolyzer (electrodes pulse), battery bank (charge bar shows the stored energy), recharge dock (contact arms lower while charging), domain beacon (head turns while active), mag rail station (clamps and carriages move when a sled docks), domain archive (data drums turn), pattern loom (heddles, beater and shuttle weave while scanning), winding post (ratchet spindle turns while winding), pressure valve (cap lifts while the boiler vents). Every motion follows the machine's real state.
- **Belts:** the belt surface moves while the belt runs and stops when it is unpowered or jammed.
- The cable terminals of the remodeled machines sit clear of their moving parts.
