# Exo Rig Boots

<ItemImage id="autarkia:exo_rig_boots" scale="2" />

The exo rig boots are the foot part of the Tier 3 power suit. They take the fall damage for FE.

## Get it

| Input | Machine | Time | Energy | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_boots" />, 3 <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_plate" />, 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:servo" />, 2 slime balls, 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:capacitor_bank" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:fabricator" /> | 200 ticks | 100 FE/t | 1 exo rig boots (empty) |

## Use it

1. Charge the boots in a <ItemLink id="autarkia:battery_bank" /> or a <ItemLink id="autarkia:capacitor_bank" />.
2. Wear them.
3. When you fall more than 3 blocks, the boots use 100 FE for each block above 3.
4. With enough FE you take no fall damage.
5. Push the suit key (default N) to switch the suit on or off.

With too little FE, the boots cover only the blocks that their FE pays for. You take damage for the rest of the fall.

Example: a fall of 23 blocks costs 2,000 FE.

## Problems

- You take fall damage: the boots have too little FE, or the suit is off.
