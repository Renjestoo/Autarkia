# Speed Upgrade

<ItemImage id="autarkia:worker_upgrade_speed" scale="2" />

The speed upgrade makes a worker walk or fly 20% faster. The worker then uses a little more energy when it moves.

## Get it

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:worker_upgrade_speed" />

## Use it

1. Hold the upgrade.
2. Use it on a worker of your colony. The upgrade is used up.
3. The action bar shows "Installed: Speed Upgrade".

Each worker has 2 upgrade slots. Two upgrades of the same type in both slots add their effects. To take all upgrades out, sneak and use a <ItemLink id="autarkia:wrench" /> on the worker. A worker that dies drops its upgrades.

## Numbers

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Speed | +20% walking or flying speed per upgrade |
| Move drain | +10% per upgrade |
| Slots per worker | 2 |

## Problems

- "Both upgrade slots are full": take the upgrades out with a sneaking wrench first.
- "Only the owner or a trusted player can upgrade this worker": the worker belongs to another player's colony.
- "Removed N upgrades": the wrench message. The upgrades go into your inventory.
