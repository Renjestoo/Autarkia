# Tier 3: Electric Works

Tier 3 brings insulated cable on steel pylons, transformers, fuse boxes, steam turbines and batteries. It also brings
pure ores, circuits and the fabricator, the warehouse with Loaders, capsule tubes, Builder Drones, Linemen and
failure memory: the colony remembers what went wrong and fixes it. Use a <ItemLink id="autarkia:core_upgrade_t3" /> on
your Colony Core to start it.
