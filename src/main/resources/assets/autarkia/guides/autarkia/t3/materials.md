# Tier 3 Materials

Tier 3 has two kinds of new materials.

- Pure ores come from the <ItemLink id="autarkia:magnetic_separator" />. Each pure ore gives 1 ingot in a furnace or in the <ItemLink id="autarkia:electrolyzer" />. They are <ItemLink id="autarkia:pure_iron" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:pure_copper" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:pure_gold" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:pure_tin" /> and <ItemLink id="autarkia:pure_zinc" />.
- Parts come from the crafting table and the <ItemLink id="autarkia:fabricator" />. The <ItemLink id="autarkia:basic_circuit" /> goes into almost every Tier 3 item. The <ItemLink id="autarkia:servo" /> goes into workers, tools and Tier 4 items.

Make the first basic circuits at the crafting table. When the fabricator runs, make them there: it gives 2 circuits for less material.
