# Basic Circuit

<ItemImage id="autarkia:basic_circuit" scale="2" />

The basic circuit is the key part of Tier 3. Almost every electric machine, every drone and every upgrade needs it. Make the first circuits by hand. Then make them in the fabricator, which gives twice as many for less material.

## Get it

At the crafting table, 1 craft gives 1 circuit:

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:basic_circuit" />

In the <ItemLink id="autarkia:fabricator" />:

| Input | Machine | Time | Energy | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:brass_plate" />, 2 redstone, 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:copper_coil" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:fabricator" /> | 200 ticks | 160 FE/t | 2 basic circuits |

## Use it

The basic circuit is an ingredient only. It goes into these items, for example:

- Power: <ItemLink id="autarkia:transformer" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:fuse_box" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:steam_turbine" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:battery_bank" />.
- Machines: <ItemLink id="autarkia:jaw_crusher" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:magnetic_separator" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:electrolyzer" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:fabricator" />.
- Logistics: <ItemLink id="autarkia:warehouse_controller" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:ledger_terminal" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:capsule_station" />.
- Workers: <ItemLink id="autarkia:drone_frame" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:recharge_dock" />, the Speed and Range upgrades, and the Builder Drone, Loader and Lineman in the worker assembler.
- Colony: <ItemLink id="autarkia:domain_beacon" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:survey_lens" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:core_upgrade_t3" /> (4 circuits).
- Parts: <ItemLink id="autarkia:servo" /> (1 circuit).
