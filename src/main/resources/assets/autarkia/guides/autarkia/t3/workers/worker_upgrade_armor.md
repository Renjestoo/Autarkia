# Armor Upgrade

<ItemImage id="autarkia:worker_upgrade_armor" scale="2" />

The armor upgrade protects a worker. It adds 6 armor points and 25% more maximum health. Use it on workers that work near monsters.

## Get it

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:worker_upgrade_armor" />

## Use it

1. Hold the upgrade.
2. Use it on a worker of your colony. The upgrade is used up.
3. The action bar shows "Installed: Armor Upgrade".

Each worker has 2 upgrade slots. To take all upgrades out, sneak and use a <ItemLink id="autarkia:wrench" /> on the worker.

## Numbers

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Armor | +6 per upgrade |
| Max health | +25% of the base health per upgrade |
| Slots per worker | 2 |

## Problems

- "Both upgrade slots are full": take the upgrades out with a sneaking wrench first.
- "Only the owner or a trusted player can upgrade this worker": the worker belongs to another player's colony.
