# Electric Workers

Tier 3 workers have an FE battery instead of a spring. Build them in the <ItemLink id="autarkia:worker_assembler" /> of a Tier 3 colony. They charge and heal at a <ItemLink id="autarkia:recharge_dock" />.

| Worker | Frame | Battery | Work |
|---|---|---|---|
| Builder Drone | <ItemLink id="autarkia:drone_frame" /> | 80,000 FE | flies; places and cuts any block, levels ground, repairs watched Patterns |
| Loader | <ItemLink id="autarkia:crab_frame" /> | 120,000 FE | carries warehouse crates between racks and ledger terminals; also fetches and carries items |
| Lineman | <ItemLink id="autarkia:walker_frame" /> | 60,000 FE | climbs poles; strings and cuts spans up to insulated cable; places poles, pylons, fuse boxes and transformers |

| Worker | Parts in the worker assembler | Time | Energy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Builder Drone | 1 drone frame, 2 basic circuits, 4 servos, 4 copper coils, 4 steel plates | 1,200 ticks | 100 FE/t |
| Loader | 1 crab frame, 2 basic circuits, 6 servos, 6 steel plates, 4 steel gears | 1,200 ticks | 100 FE/t |
| Lineman | 1 walker frame, 1 basic circuit, 4 servos, 1 insulated cable spool, 2 steel plates | 1,000 ticks | 80 FE/t |

A Tier 3 colony can have at most 4 Builder Drones, 2 Loaders and 2 Linemen.

Four upgrades improve a worker. Each worker has 2 upgrade slots: <ItemLink id="autarkia:worker_upgrade_speed" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:worker_upgrade_cargo" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:worker_upgrade_armor" /> and <ItemLink id="autarkia:worker_upgrade_range" />.
