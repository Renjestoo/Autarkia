# Pure Ores and Circuits

The ore line of tier 3 has four steps:

1. The <ItemLink id="autarkia:jaw_crusher" /> (2 x 2 x 2, on foundation, 192 FE/t) turns 1 raw ore into 2 crushed ore.
2. The sluice washes the crushed ore.
3. The <ItemLink id="autarkia:magnetic_separator" /> turns 2 washed ore into 3 pure ore, 1 slag and sometimes a nugget.
4. The <ItemLink id="autarkia:electrolyzer" /> turns 1 pure ore into 1 ingot. A furnace also smelts pure ore.

## Fabricator

The <ItemLink id="autarkia:fabricator" /> makes <ItemLink id="autarkia:basic_circuit" />,
<ItemLink id="autarkia:servo" />, frames, upgrades and the electric equipment. Select the target recipe with the
arrow buttons. Then put the ingredients in its 3 x 3 buffer.
