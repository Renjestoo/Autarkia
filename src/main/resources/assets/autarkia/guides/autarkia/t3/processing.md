# Pure Ores and Circuits

The Tier 3 machines run on FE. They give more metal per ore and make the electric parts.

The ore line has four steps. One raw ore gives 3 ingots.

1. The <ItemLink id="autarkia:jaw_crusher" /> turns 1 raw ore into 2 crushed ore. It stands on foundation.
2. The <ItemLink id="autarkia:sluice" /> washes the crushed ore.
3. The <ItemLink id="autarkia:magnetic_separator" /> turns 2 washed ore into 3 pure ore, 1 slag and sometimes a nugget.
4. The <ItemLink id="autarkia:electrolyzer" /> turns 1 pure ore into 1 ingot and sometimes a nugget. A furnace also smelts pure ore.

The <ItemLink id="autarkia:fabricator" /> makes the parts of Tier 3: basic circuits, servos, the Core Upgrade T3 and the electric equipment. Select a target recipe, then fill its 3 x 3 buffer.

Connect each machine to the insulated grid. Put a node next to it, or string a span to its ceramic bushing.
