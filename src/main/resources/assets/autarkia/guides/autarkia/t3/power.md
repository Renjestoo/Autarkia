# Insulated Grid

Tier 3 machines need much more energy than Tier 2 machines. The insulated grid carries it.

- <ItemLink id="autarkia:insulated_cable_spool" /> carries 1,024 FE/t over spans of up to 24 blocks.
- <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_pylon" /> holds 6 spans. <ItemLink id="autarkia:wall_insulator" /> holds 2 spans on a wall. Machines next to them join the network.
- <ItemLink id="autarkia:transformer" /> joins your copper wire grid to the insulated grid.
- <ItemLink id="autarkia:fuse_box" /> opens a line before an overload makes a cable snap.
- <ItemLink id="autarkia:battery_bank" /> stores 400,000 FE and charges electric tools.
- <ItemLink id="autarkia:steam_turbine" /> makes up to 1,000 FE/t from the steam of up to 4 boilers.

A good first grid: boilers feed a steam turbine. A pylon next to the turbine starts the insulated line. A fuse box sits on the line, and battery banks stand next to a pylon. A transformer feeds the old copper grid.
