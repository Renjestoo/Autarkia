# Survey Lens

<ItemImage id="autarkia:survey_lens" scale="2" />

The survey lens shows the ore deposits around you through the ground. Use it to find ore before you dig or before you place a domain beacon.

## Get it

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:survey_lens" />

## Use it

1. Hold the lens in your main hand or in your off hand.
2. Look around. Each deposit within 64 blocks shows as an outline box. The outlines show through blocks.
3. Walk on. The lens scans again every 2 seconds.
4. Put the lens away to remove the outlines.

A deposit is a group of touching ore blocks of one kind. The lens shows the nearest 256 deposits.

## Colors

| Ore | Outline color |
|---|---|
| Iron | light brown |
| Copper | orange |
| Gold | yellow |
| Coal | dark gray |
| Diamond | light blue |
| Redstone | red |
| Lapis | blue |
| Emerald | green |
| Other ores, for example tin and zinc | white |

## Numbers

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Range | 64 blocks |
| Scan | every 2 seconds |
| Deposits shown | 256 at most |
| Stack size | 1 |
