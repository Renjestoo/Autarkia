# Pure Tin Ore

<ItemImage id="autarkia:pure_tin" scale="2" />

Pure tin ore is the clean ore of the Tier 3 ore line. Each piece gives 1 tin ingot. One raw tin gives 3 pure tin ore through the full line.

## Get it

The <ItemLink id="autarkia:magnetic_separator" /> makes it. No world generation and no crafting recipe make pure ore.

| Input | Machine | Time | Energy | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 <ItemLink id="autarkia:washed_tin" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:magnetic_separator" /> | 120 ticks | 128 FE/t | 3 pure tin ore, 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:slag" />, 1 iron nugget at 50% |

The full line is: <ItemLink id="autarkia:raw_tin" /> in the <ItemLink id="autarkia:jaw_crusher" /> (2 crushed tin), crushed tin in the <ItemLink id="autarkia:sluice" /> (washed tin), washed tin in the separator.

## Use it

| Input | Machine | Time | Energy | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 pure tin ore | furnace | 200 ticks | none | 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:tin_ingot" /> |
| 1 pure tin ore | blast furnace | 100 ticks | none | 1 tin ingot |
| 1 pure tin ore | <ItemLink id="autarkia:electrolyzer" /> | 160 ticks | 256 FE/t | 1 tin ingot, 1 iron nugget at 10% |
