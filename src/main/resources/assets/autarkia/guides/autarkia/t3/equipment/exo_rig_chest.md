# Exo Rig Chestplate

<ItemImage id="autarkia:exo_rig_chest" scale="2" />

The exo rig chestplate is the body part of the Tier 3 power suit. It gives strong armor and makes you jump higher.

## Get it

| Input | Machine | Time | Energy | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_chest" />, 6 <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_plate" />, 2 <ItemLink id="autarkia:servo" />, 2 <ItemLink id="autarkia:basic_circuit" />, 2 pistons, 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:capacitor_bank" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:fabricator" /> | 300 ticks | 134 FE/t | 1 exo rig chestplate (empty) |

## Use it

1. Charge the chestplate in a <ItemLink id="autarkia:battery_bank" /> or a <ItemLink id="autarkia:capacitor_bank" />.
2. Wear it.
3. Jump. Each jump uses 100 FE.
4. Push the suit key (default N) to switch the suit on or off.

The jump boost works while the suit is on and the chestplate has 100 FE or more.

## Problems

- The jump is normal: the chestplate has less than 100 FE, or the suit is off.
- Wear the <ItemLink id="autarkia:exo_rig_helmet" /> too. It moves FE from the other parts into the chestplate.
