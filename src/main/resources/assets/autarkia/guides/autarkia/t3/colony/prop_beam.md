# Prop Beam

<ItemImage id="autarkia:prop_beam" scale="2" />

The prop beam is a simple wooden support block. The colony workers use it in a Rack Upgrade project: they put a prop beam under each rack of a failing row that stands on nothing. Then they replace the wood racks with steel racks.

## Get it

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:prop_beam" />

One craft gives 4 prop beams.

## Use it

- Keep some prop beams in colony storage. The Rack Upgrade project needs them.
- You can also place a prop beam under a rack yourself. A rack needs a sturdy block or another rack below it.
- Break it with an axe. It drops itself.

## When the colony uses it

The colony proposes a Rack Upgrade when a rack collapsed, or when a wood rack stays at 85% load or more. The project:

1. Puts a prop beam under each rack of the row that has no support.
2. Removes the wood racks of the row.
3. Places steel racks at the same places, also at the places of collapsed racks.

The crates of a removed rack drop. The workers put them back into racks.
