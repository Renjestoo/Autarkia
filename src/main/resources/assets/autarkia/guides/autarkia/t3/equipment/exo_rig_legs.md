# Exo Rig Leggings

<ItemImage id="autarkia:exo_rig_legs" scale="2" />

The exo rig leggings are the leg part of the Tier 3 power suit. They let you walk up full blocks without a jump.

## Get it

| Input | Machine | Time | Energy | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_legs" />, 5 <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_plate" />, 2 <ItemLink id="autarkia:servo" />, 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:basic_circuit" />, 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:capacitor_bank" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:fabricator" /> | 250 ticks | 120 FE/t | 1 exo rig leggings (empty) |

## Use it

1. Charge the leggings in a <ItemLink id="autarkia:battery_bank" /> or a <ItemLink id="autarkia:capacitor_bank" />.
2. Wear them.
3. Walk against a block that is 1 block high. You step up on it.
4. Push the suit key (default N) to switch the suit on or off.

The leggings use FE only while you walk on the ground.

## Problems

- You do not step up: the leggings have 0 FE, or the suit is off.
