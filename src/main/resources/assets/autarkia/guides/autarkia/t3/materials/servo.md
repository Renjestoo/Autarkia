# Servo

<ItemImage id="autarkia:servo" scale="2" />

The servo is the motor part of Tier 3. Electric workers, electric tools and many Tier 4 items need it. Only the fabricator makes it.

## Get it

| Input | Machine | Time | Energy | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_gear" />, 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:copper_coil" />, 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_plate" />, 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:basic_circuit" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:fabricator" /> | 200 ticks | 160 FE/t | 1 servo |
