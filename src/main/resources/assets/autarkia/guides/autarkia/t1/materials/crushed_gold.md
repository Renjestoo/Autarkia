# Crushed Gold Ore

<ItemImage id="autarkia:crushed_gold" scale="2" />

Crushed gold ore is ground raw gold. One raw gold gives 1.5 crushed gold on average, and each crushed gold smelts to
one ingot.

## Get it

| Input | Machine | Time | Outputs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:raw_gold" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:quern" /> | 8 turns | 1 crushed gold, 1 more at 50% |
| 1 raw gold | <ItemLink id="autarkia:stamp_mill" /> | 160 ticks at 16 FE/t | 1 crushed gold, 1 more at 50% |
| 1 raw gold | Jaw crusher (Tier 3) | 100 ticks at 192 FE/t | 2 crushed gold |

## Use it

| Process | Machine | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Smelting | Furnace, 200 ticks | 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:gold_ingot" /> |
| Blasting | Blast furnace, 100 ticks | 1 gold ingot |
| Washing | Sluice (Tier 2) | 1 washed gold ore, 1 copper nugget at 10% |
| Flotation | Froth flotation plant (Tier 4) | 2 gold concentrate, slag at 50%, 1 copper nugget at 35% |
