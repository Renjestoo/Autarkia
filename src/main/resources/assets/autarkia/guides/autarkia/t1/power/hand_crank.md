# Hand Crank

<ItemImage id="autarkia:hand_crank" scale="2" />

The hand crank is your first generator. You turn it with your hand, and it makes FE (energy) for your first machines.
A Porter can turn it too.

## Get it

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:hand_crank" />

## Use it

1. Place the crank near the machine that needs power.
2. Hold a <ItemLink id="autarkia:rope_coil" /> and use it on the crank.
3. Use the rope coil on the machine or on a <ItemLink id="autarkia:rope_pulley" />. This makes a rope span.
4. Hold use on the crank with an empty hand. The crank turns and makes 20 FE/t.
5. Let go to stop. The output stops 6 ticks later.

Cranking makes you hungry. Eat food when your hunger bar goes down.

## Numbers

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Output, player | 20 FE/t |
| Output, Porter | 10 FE/t |
| Buffer | 1,000 FE |
| Maximum output to the network | 40 FE/t |
| Run time after you let go | 6 ticks |
| Rope spans on the crank | 2 |
| Mining tool | Pickaxe |
