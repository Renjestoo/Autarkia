# Crate

<ItemImage id="autarkia:crate" scale="2" />

The crate is the storage block of Tier 1. It holds 27 stacks. Piles of its items show on its top, so you can see the
contents from outside. The colony takes its building materials from the crates in its domain.

## Get it

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:crate" />

## Use it

1. Place the crate inside the domain of your colony.
2. Open it and put your items in.
3. To move a full crate, sneak and use a <ItemLink id="autarkia:wrench" /> on it. You get the crate as an item with all
   its contents inside.
4. Place the crate item again. The contents come back.

When you break a crate without a wrench, it drops its contents.

## Numbers

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Slots | 27 |
| Fill shown in Jade | Used slots of 27 |
| Mining tool | Axe |

## Problems

- You cannot put a filled crate item into a crate or a chute. Place the filled crate first.
- The colony proposes a Crate Cluster: the crates of the domain were 85% full for 30 seconds. Approve the project, or
  empty some crates.
