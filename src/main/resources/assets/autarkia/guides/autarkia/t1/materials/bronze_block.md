# Block of Bronze

<ItemImage id="autarkia:bronze_block" scale="2" />

The block of bronze stores 9 bronze ingots in one block. Two Tier 1 recipes also need it as a part.

## Get it

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:bronze_block" />

## Use it

Blocks of bronze go into:

- The <ItemLink id="autarkia:stone_flywheel" />: 1 block.
- The <ItemLink id="autarkia:worker_assembler" />: 5 blocks.

To get the ingots back, put the block into a crafting grid. You get 9 <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_ingot" />.

## Numbers

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Contents | 9 bronze ingots |
| Tool | Stone pickaxe or better |
