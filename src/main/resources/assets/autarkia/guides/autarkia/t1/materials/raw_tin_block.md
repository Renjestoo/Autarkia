# Block of Raw Tin

<ItemImage id="autarkia:raw_tin_block" scale="2" />

The block of raw tin stores 9 raw tin in one block. It saves space in your crates and chests.

## Get it

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:raw_tin_block" />

## Use it

1. Place it as a building block, or keep it in storage.
2. To get the raw tin back, put the block into a crafting grid. You get 9 <ItemLink id="autarkia:raw_tin" />.

## Numbers

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Contents | 9 raw tin |
| Tool | Stone pickaxe or better |
