# Tin Nugget

<ItemImage id="autarkia:tin_nugget" scale="2" />

The tin nugget is a small piece of tin. Nine nuggets make one tin ingot.

## Get it

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:tin_nugget" />

1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:tin_ingot" /> gives 9 tin nuggets.

## Use it

1. Put 9 tin nuggets into a crafting grid to get 1 tin ingot.
2. Load a nugget into the <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_sling" />. The sling throws any nugget for 4 damage.
