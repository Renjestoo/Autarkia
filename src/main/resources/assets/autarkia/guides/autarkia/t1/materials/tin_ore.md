# Tin Ore

<ItemImage id="autarkia:tin_ore" scale="2" />

Tin ore is the first new ore of Autarkia. Tin and copper make bronze, the metal of Tier 1. Find tin ore before you
do anything else.

## Get it

Tin ore generates in stone in all Overworld biomes:

| Vein type | Y range | Vein size | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small vein | -16 to 96, most near Y 40 | 9 | 12 tries in each chunk |
| Big deposit | 0 to 64 | 20 | 1 in 6 chunks |

A big deposit with 12 or more ore blocks puts an ore outcrop (a small rock pile) on the surface above it. Look for
these rock piles. Where a vein replaces deepslate, it places <ItemLink id="autarkia:deepslate_tin_ore" />.

## Use it

1. Mine the ore with a stone pickaxe or better.
2. You get 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:raw_tin" />. Fortune gives more.
3. With Silk Touch you get the ore block. You can smelt the ore block directly to a tin ingot.

## Numbers

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Hardness | 3.0 |
| Tool | Stone pickaxe or better |
| Drop | 1 raw tin (Fortune adds more) |
| Silk Touch drop | Tin Ore |
| Furnace | 1 tin ingot in 200 ticks |
| Blast furnace | 1 tin ingot in 100 ticks |
