# Mainspring

<ItemImage id="autarkia:mainspring" scale="2" />

The mainspring is a coiled iron spring. Spring workers, such as the Porter, run on its stored tension.

## Get it

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:mainspring" />

4 iron ingots make 1 mainspring.

## Use it

Mainsprings go into:

- The Porter in the <ItemLink id="autarkia:worker_assembler" />: 2 mainsprings for each Porter.
- The <ItemLink id="autarkia:winding_post" />, which winds the springs of the workers.
- The <ItemLink id="autarkia:worker_capsule" />, which stores a worker.
- The Hauler Crab, a worker of Tier 2.
