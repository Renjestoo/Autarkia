# Raw Tin

<ItemImage id="autarkia:raw_tin" scale="2" />

Raw tin is the drop of tin ore. Smelt it to a tin ingot, or grind it first for more ingots.

## Get it

- Mine <ItemLink id="autarkia:tin_ore" /> or <ItemLink id="autarkia:deepslate_tin_ore" /> with a stone pickaxe or
  better.
- Break an ore outcrop of tin (a small rock pile on the surface). It gives 1 raw tin.
- Craft 9 raw tin from a <ItemLink id="autarkia:raw_tin_block" />:

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:raw_tin" />

## Use it

| Process | Machine | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Smelting | Furnace, 200 ticks | 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:tin_ingot" /> |
| Blasting | Blast furnace, 100 ticks | 1 tin ingot |
| Crushing | <ItemLink id="autarkia:quern" />, 8 turns | 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:crushed_tin" />, 1 more at 50% |
| Crushing | <ItemLink id="autarkia:stamp_mill" />, 160 ticks at 16 FE/t | 1 crushed tin, 1 more at 50% |
| Storage | Crafting table | 9 raw tin make 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:raw_tin_block" /> |

Crush the raw tin before you smelt it. On average you get 1.5 ingots instead of 1.
