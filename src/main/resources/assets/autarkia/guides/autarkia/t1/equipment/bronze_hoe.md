# Bronze Hoe

<ItemImage id="autarkia:bronze_hoe" scale="2" />

The bronze hoe tills soil for farms. It also breaks leaves, hay and other hoe blocks fast.

## Get it

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:bronze_hoe" />

You need 2 <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_ingot" /> and 2 sticks.

## Use it

1. Hold the hoe in your main hand.
2. Use the hoe on dirt or grass to make farmland.
3. Plant seeds on the farmland.
4. To repair the hoe, put it in an anvil with a bronze ingot.
