# Tin and Bronze

Tier 1 runs on bronze. You make bronze from copper and tin. Copper is a vanilla ore. Tin comes from
<ItemLink id="autarkia:tin_ore" /> and <ItemLink id="autarkia:deepslate_tin_ore" />.

## How the materials work together

1. Mine tin ore to get <ItemLink id="autarkia:raw_tin" />.
2. Smelt raw tin to a <ItemLink id="autarkia:tin_ingot" />. Or grind it first to
   <ItemLink id="autarkia:crushed_tin" /> for more ingots.
3. Alloy 3 copper ingots and 1 tin ingot in the crucible to 4 <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_ingot" />.
4. Craft bronze ingots into <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_gear" />. Almost every Tier 1 machine needs gears.
5. Craft iron ingots into a <ItemLink id="autarkia:mainspring" /> for worker parts.

Crushed ore from the quern or the stamp mill gives more ingots: 1 raw ore gives 1 crushed ore, and 1 more at a chance
of 50%. This works for <ItemLink id="autarkia:crushed_copper" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:crushed_iron" />,
<ItemLink id="autarkia:crushed_gold" /> and crushed tin.

## Storage forms

Nine nuggets make one ingot. Nine ingots make one block.

- <ItemLink id="autarkia:tin_nugget" /> and <ItemLink id="autarkia:tin_block" />
- <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_nugget" /> and <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_block" />
- <ItemLink id="autarkia:raw_tin_block" /> holds 9 raw tin.
