# Tier 1: Rope and Bronze

Tier 1 has no electricity in wires. Power comes from your arms, from animals and from the wind. Rope drives carry it
to the machines. Bronze is the first metal you make.

At the end of Tier 1 you plant a Colony Core. The colony then builds windmills, treadwheels, crates and winding posts
by itself, with Porters.
