# Bronze Ingot

<ItemImage id="autarkia:bronze_ingot" scale="2" />

Bronze is the metal of Tier 1. It is an alloy of copper and tin. Almost every Tier 1 machine, tool and worker part
needs bronze.

## Get it

| Inputs | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 <ItemLink id="minecraft:copper_ingot" /> + 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:tin_ingot" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:crucible" /> | 300 ticks at 1.0x heat | 4 bronze ingots |
| 3 copper ingots + 1 tin ingot | Blast smelter (Tier 2) | - | 4 bronze ingots |

You can also craft bronze ingots back from nuggets or a block:

<RecipesFor id="autarkia:bronze_ingot" />

## Use it

Bronze ingots go into:

- <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_gear" /> and <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_nugget" />
- <ItemLink id="autarkia:windmill" /> and <ItemLink id="autarkia:stamp_mill" />
- <ItemLink id="autarkia:core_seed" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:walker_frame" />,
  <ItemLink id="autarkia:worker_capsule" /> and <ItemLink id="autarkia:planning_tablet" />
- The Porter in the <ItemLink id="autarkia:worker_assembler" />: 4 bronze ingots for each Porter
- <ItemLink id="autarkia:wrench" />, bronze tools and bronze armor
- <ItemLink id="autarkia:core_upgrade_t2" />
- <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_block" /> for storage

Bronze ingots also repair bronze armor.
