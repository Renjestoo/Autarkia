# Bronze Helmet

<ItemImage id="autarkia:bronze_helmet" scale="2" />

The bronze helmet is the head piece of the Tier 1 armor set. It gives 2 armor points.

## Get it

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:bronze_helmet" />

You need 5 <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_ingot" />.

## Use it

1. Put the helmet in the head slot of your inventory. You can also hold it and press use.
2. To repair the helmet, put it in an anvil with a bronze ingot.
