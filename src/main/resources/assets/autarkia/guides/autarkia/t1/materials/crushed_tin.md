# Crushed Tin Ore

<ItemImage id="autarkia:crushed_tin" scale="2" />

Crushed tin ore is ground raw tin. One raw tin gives 1.5 crushed tin on average, and each crushed tin smelts to one
ingot. So crushing gives you more tin.

## Get it

| Input | Machine | Time | Outputs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:raw_tin" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:quern" /> | 8 turns | 1 crushed tin, 1 more at 50% |
| 1 raw tin | <ItemLink id="autarkia:stamp_mill" /> | 160 ticks at 16 FE/t | 1 crushed tin, 1 more at 50% |
| 1 raw tin | Jaw crusher (Tier 3) | 100 ticks at 192 FE/t | 2 crushed tin |

## Use it

| Process | Machine | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Smelting | Furnace, 200 ticks | 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:tin_ingot" /> |
| Blasting | Blast furnace, 100 ticks | 1 tin ingot |
| Washing | Sluice (Tier 2) | 1 washed tin ore, 1 iron nugget at 10% |
| Flotation | Froth flotation plant (Tier 4) | 2 tin concentrate, slag at 50%, 1 iron nugget at 35% |
