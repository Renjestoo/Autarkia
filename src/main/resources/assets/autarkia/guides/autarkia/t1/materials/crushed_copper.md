# Crushed Copper Ore

<ItemImage id="autarkia:crushed_copper" scale="2" />

Crushed copper ore is ground raw copper. One raw copper gives 1.5 crushed copper on average, and each crushed copper
smelts to one ingot. Bronze needs 3 copper for each tin, so crush your copper.

## Get it

| Input | Machine | Time | Outputs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:raw_copper" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:quern" /> | 8 turns | 1 crushed copper, 1 more at 50% |
| 1 raw copper | <ItemLink id="autarkia:stamp_mill" /> | 160 ticks at 16 FE/t | 1 crushed copper, 1 more at 50% |
| 1 raw copper | Jaw crusher (Tier 3) | 100 ticks at 192 FE/t | 2 crushed copper |

## Use it

| Process | Machine | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Smelting | Furnace, 200 ticks | 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:copper_ingot" /> |
| Blasting | Blast furnace, 100 ticks | 1 copper ingot |
| Washing | Sluice (Tier 2) | 1 washed copper ore, 1 gold nugget at 10% |
| Flotation | Froth flotation plant (Tier 4) | 2 copper concentrate, slag at 50%, 1 gold nugget at 35% |
