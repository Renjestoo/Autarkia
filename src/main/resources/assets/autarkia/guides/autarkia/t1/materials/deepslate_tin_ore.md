# Deepslate Tin Ore

<ItemImage id="autarkia:deepslate_tin_ore" scale="2" />

Deepslate tin ore is tin ore in deepslate. It gives the same raw tin as <ItemLink id="autarkia:tin_ore" />. It is
harder, so it takes longer to mine.

## Get it

The tin veins place this ore where they replace deepslate. The small veins generate from Y -16 to Y 96. The big
deposits generate from Y 0 to Y 64. Deepslate is in the low part of these ranges.

## Use it

1. Mine the ore with a stone pickaxe or better.
2. You get 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:raw_tin" />. Fortune gives more.
3. With Silk Touch you get the ore block. You can smelt it directly to a tin ingot.

## Numbers

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Hardness | 4.5 |
| Tool | Stone pickaxe or better |
| Drop | 1 raw tin (Fortune adds more) |
| Silk Touch drop | Deepslate Tin Ore |
| Furnace | 1 tin ingot in 200 ticks |
| Blast furnace | 1 tin ingot in 100 ticks |
