# Bronze Boots

<ItemImage id="autarkia:bronze_boots" scale="2" />

The bronze boots are the foot piece of the Tier 1 armor set. They give 2 armor points.

## Get it

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:bronze_boots" />

You need 4 <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_ingot" />.

## Use it

1. Put the boots in the feet slot of your inventory. You can also hold them and press use.
2. To repair the boots, put them in an anvil with a bronze ingot.

## Numbers

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Armor | 2 |
| Armor toughness | 0 |
| Knockback resistance | 0 |
| Durability | 182 |

| Repair item | Bronze Ingot |
