# Bronze Chestplate

<ItemImage id="autarkia:bronze_chest" scale="2" />

The bronze chestplate is the body piece of the Tier 1 armor set. It gives 5 armor points, the most of the four pieces.

## Get it

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:bronze_chest" />

You need 8 <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_ingot" />.

## Use it

1. Put the chestplate in the chest slot of your inventory. You can also hold it and press use.
2. To repair the chestplate, put it in an anvil with a bronze ingot.

## Numbers

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Armor | 5 |
| Armor toughness | 0 |
| Knockback resistance | 0 |
| Durability | 224 |
| Enchantability | 12 |
| Repair item | Bronze Ingot |
| Full set | 13 armor (helmet 2, chestplate 5, leggings 4, boots 2) |
