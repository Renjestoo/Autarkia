# Field Manual

<ItemImage id="autarkia:field_manual" scale="2" />

The field manual is this guide as an item. It explains the tiers, the machines, the colony and its workers.

## Get it

- You get one field manual the first time you join a world.
- You get one more when your first Colony Core blooms.
- You can craft one from a book and a <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_nugget" />:

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:field_manual" />

## Use it

1. Hold the field manual.
2. Press use. The manual opens on its start page.
3. To open the page of one item, point at the item in your inventory and hold the guide key (default G).

## Problems

- You did not get a manual when you joined: the server option `colony.starterManual` is off. Craft one.
