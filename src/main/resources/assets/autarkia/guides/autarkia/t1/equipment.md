# Bronze Equipment

Bronze tools mine what iron tools mine. They have speed 6.5 and 300 uses. A full set of bronze armor gives 13 armor
points.

The <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_sling" /> throws cobblestone (3 damage) or any nugget (4 damage). Hold use for 5 to
20 ticks, then let go.
