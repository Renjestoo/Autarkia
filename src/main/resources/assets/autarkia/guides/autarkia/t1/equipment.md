# Bronze Equipment

Bronze is the first Autarkia metal. A <ItemLink id="autarkia:crucible" /> melts 3 copper ingots and 1
<ItemLink id="autarkia:tin_ingot" /> into 4 bronze ingots. Bronze tools are a little better than iron tools. Bronze
armor is a little weaker than iron armor, but you can make it before you find much iron.

## Tools

All bronze tools mine what iron tools mine. They have 300 uses and a mining speed of 6.5. Repair them in an anvil
with a <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_ingot" />.

| Tool | Attack damage | Attack speed | Special use |
|---|---|---|---|
| <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_pickaxe" /> | 4 | 1.2 | Stone and ores |
| <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_axe" /> | 9 | 0.9 | Strips logs, stops a shield for 5 seconds |
| <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_shovel" /> | 4.5 | 1.0 | Makes paths |
| <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_hoe" /> | 1 | 3.0 | Tills soil |
| <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_sword" /> | 6 | 1.6 | Sweep attack |

## Ranged weapon

The <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_sling" /> throws cobblestone (3 damage) or any nugget (4 damage). It has 250 uses.
Repair it with string.

## Armor

A full set of bronze armor gives 13 armor points (a full iron set gives 15). Bronze armor has no toughness. Repair it in an anvil with a bronze
ingot.

| Piece | Armor | Durability |
|---|---|---|
| <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_helmet" /> | 2 | 154 |
| <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_chest" /> | 5 | 224 |
| <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_legs" /> | 4 | 210 |
| <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_boots" /> | 2 | 182 |
