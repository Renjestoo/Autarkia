# Bronze Axe

<ItemImage id="autarkia:bronze_axe" scale="2" />

The bronze axe cuts wood fast and hits hard. A hit with the axe also stops a shield from blocking for 5 seconds.

## Get it

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:bronze_axe" />

You need 3 <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_ingot" /> and 2 sticks.

## Use it

1. Hold the axe in your main hand.
2. Cut logs, planks and other wood blocks.
3. Use the axe on a log to strip the bark.
4. Hit a mob that holds up a shield. The shield stops blocking for 5 seconds.
5. To repair the axe, put it in an anvil with a bronze ingot.
