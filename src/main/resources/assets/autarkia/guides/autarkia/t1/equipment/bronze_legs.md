# Bronze Leggings

<ItemImage id="autarkia:bronze_legs" scale="2" />

The bronze leggings are the leg piece of the Tier 1 armor set. They give 4 armor points.

## Get it

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:bronze_legs" />

You need 7 <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_ingot" />.

## Use it

1. Put the leggings in the legs slot of your inventory. You can also hold them and press use.
2. To repair the leggings, put them in an anvil with a bronze ingot.

## Numbers

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Armor | 4 |
| Armor toughness | 0 |
| Knockback resistance | 0 |
| Durability | 210 |
| Enchantability | 12 |
| Repair item | Bronze Ingot |
| Full set | 13 armor (helmet 2, chestplate 5, leggings 4, boots 2) |
