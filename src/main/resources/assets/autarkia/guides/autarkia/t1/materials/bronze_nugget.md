# Bronze Nugget

<ItemImage id="autarkia:bronze_nugget" scale="2" />

The bronze nugget is a small piece of bronze. Nine nuggets make one bronze ingot. Some cheap Tier 1 recipes need only
one nugget.

## Get it

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:bronze_nugget" />

1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_ingot" /> gives 9 bronze nuggets.

## Use it

Bronze nuggets go into:

- The <ItemLink id="autarkia:crate" />: 1 nugget.
- The <ItemLink id="autarkia:chute" />: 1 nugget for 4 chutes.
- The <ItemLink id="autarkia:field_manual" />: 1 nugget and a book.
- 9 nuggets make 1 bronze ingot again.

The <ItemLink id="autarkia:bronze_sling" /> throws any nugget for 4 damage.
