# Autarkia Field Manual

Autarkia is a factory that builds itself. You start with muscle power and rope. Then you plant a Colony Core. The
colony reads its sensors, finds what it needs, and its workers build it.

Each tier adds machines, power and new workers. This manual has one chapter per tier.

## First steps

1. Find tin ore. Make bronze in a <ItemLink id="autarkia:crucible" />.
2. Turn a <ItemLink id="autarkia:hand_crank" /> and send the power over rope to a <ItemLink id="autarkia:stamp_mill" />.
3. Craft a <ItemLink id="autarkia:core_seed" /> and plant it.
4. Watch the colony propose its first project.
