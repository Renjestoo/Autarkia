# Autarkia Field Manual

Autarkia is a factory that builds itself. You start with muscle power and rope. Then you plant a Colony Core. The
colony reads its sensors, finds what it needs, and its workers build it. Each tier adds machines, power and workers.

## Use this manual

- Hold an Autarkia item in your inventory and press the guide key (default G). The manual opens on the page of that
  item.
- Every item, block, machine, tool and armor piece has its own page.
- Each tier chapter starts with a step-by-step progression. Follow the steps in order.
- The **Autarkia: Getting Started** advancement tab also shows the next step.

## Progression

1. **Tier 1: Rope and Bronze.** Find tin ore. Make bronze in a <ItemLink id="autarkia:crucible" />. Turn a
   <ItemLink id="autarkia:hand_crank" /> and carry the power over rope to a <ItemLink id="autarkia:stamp_mill" />.
   Craft a <ItemLink id="autarkia:core_seed" /> and plant it. The colony starts to build.
2. **Tier 2: Steam and Steel.** Use a <ItemLink id="autarkia:core_upgrade_t2" /> on the Colony Core. Make brass and
   steel. Build steam power and run copper wire on poles.
3. **Tier 3: Wires and Circuits.** Use a <ItemLink id="autarkia:core_upgrade_t3" />. Build the electric grid with
   transformers, make circuits in the fabricator, and start the warehouse and the capsule network.
4. **Tier 4: Outposts.** Use a <ItemLink id="autarkia:core_upgrade_t4" />. Mine vantite, build outposts far from the
   base, and move goods by cable car and crate launcher.
5. **Tier 5: Lattice and Nanites.** Use a <ItemLink id="autarkia:core_upgrade_t5" />. Grow lattice crystals, run
   superconductors and plasma power, and let replicators and nanites work.
6. **Tier 6: Autarky.** Use a <ItemLink id="autarkia:core_upgrade_t6" />. Compile matter, build the Seed Matrix and
   make the Autarky Seed.
