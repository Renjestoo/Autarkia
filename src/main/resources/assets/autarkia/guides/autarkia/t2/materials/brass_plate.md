# Brass Plate

<ItemImage id="autarkia:brass_plate" scale="2" />

The brass plate is a flat sheet of brass. Many Tier 2 machines and parts need it.

## Get it

| Input | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:brass_ingot" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:plate_press" /> (48 FE/t) | 80 ticks (4 s) | 1 brass plate |

## Use it

1. Put brass ingots in the plate press.
2. Take the brass plates from the output slot.
3. Use the plates in the crafting recipes below.
