# Tier 2: Steam and Copper

Tier 2 brings copper wire on wooden poles, water and steam power, and the first danger of electricity: hot wires
and lightning. It also brings zinc, brass and steel, belts, racks that can collapse, the Hauler Crab and Patterns.
Use a <ItemLink id="autarkia:core_upgrade_t2" /> on your Colony Core to start it.
