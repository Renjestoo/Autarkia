# Washed Iron Ore

<ItemImage id="autarkia:washed_iron" scale="2" />

Washed iron ore is crushed iron ore that the sluice cleaned with water. In the blast smelter, 3 washed ore
make 4 ingots. That is the best Tier 2 yield.

## Get it

| Input | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:crushed_iron" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:sluice" /> | 100 ticks with flowing water | 1 washed iron ore, plus 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:zinc_nugget" /> at 10% |

Crush raw iron in a <ItemLink id="autarkia:quern" /> or a <ItemLink id="autarkia:stamp_mill" /> first.

## Use it

| Input | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 washed iron ore | <ItemLink id="autarkia:blast_smelter" /> | 200 ticks (10 s) | 4 iron ingots, plus 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:slag" /> at 30% |
| 1 washed iron ore | Furnace or blast furnace | 200 or 100 ticks | 1 iron ingot |
| 2 washed iron ore | <ItemLink id="autarkia:magnetic_separator" /> (Tier 3) | 120 ticks | 3 pure iron ore, 1 slag, plus 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:zinc_nugget" /> at 50% |

1. Put the washed ore in one input slot of the blast smelter. Keep the other input slots empty.
2. Wait until the smelter is hot and works.
3. Take the ingots and the slag from the output slots.
