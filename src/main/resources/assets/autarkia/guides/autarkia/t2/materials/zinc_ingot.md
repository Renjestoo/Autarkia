# Zinc Ingot

<ItemImage id="autarkia:zinc_ingot" scale="2" />

The zinc ingot is the Tier 2 metal. Its main use is brass: 3 copper ingots and 1 zinc ingot make 4 brass ingots.

## Get it

| Input | Machine | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:raw_zinc" />, zinc ore or deepslate zinc ore | Furnace (200 ticks) or blast furnace (100 ticks) | 1 zinc ingot |
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:crushed_zinc" /> | Furnace (200 ticks) or blast furnace (100 ticks) | 1 zinc ingot |
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:washed_zinc" /> | Furnace (200 ticks) or blast furnace (100 ticks) | 1 zinc ingot |
| 3 washed zinc | <ItemLink id="autarkia:blast_smelter" /> (200 ticks) | 4 zinc ingots, slag at 30% |

You can also craft 9 <ItemLink id="autarkia:zinc_nugget" /> into 1 ingot, or 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:zinc_block" />
into 9 ingots.

## Use it

1. Put 3 copper ingots and 1 zinc ingot in a <ItemLink id="autarkia:crucible" />. You get 4
   <ItemLink id="autarkia:brass_ingot" />.
2. Or do the same in the blast smelter. Put the copper and the zinc in two different input slots.
