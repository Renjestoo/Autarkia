# Catwalk

<ItemImage id="autarkia:catwalk" scale="2" />

The catwalk is a see-through steel grate. You can walk on it. Use it for walkways around your boilers, poles and
machines. You can see the machines and the wires through it.

## Get it

One craft gives 4 catwalks.

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:catwalk" />

## Use it

1. Place catwalks as a floor or a bridge.
2. Connect two levels with <ItemLink id="autarkia:catwalk_stairs" />.

The catwalk is not a full block for light and for redstone. It does not carry a redstone signal, and you cannot
suffocate in it.

## Numbers

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Hardness | 2 |
| Blast resistance | 6 |
