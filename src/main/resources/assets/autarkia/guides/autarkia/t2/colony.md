# The T2 Colony

Use a <ItemLink id="autarkia:core_upgrade_t2" /> on an active T1 Core. The Core upgrades for 5 seconds. Then the domain
is 49 x 49 blocks, the colony can have 8 workers, 2 active projects and a bigger daily budget.

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:core_upgrade_t2" />

## New projects

| Project | When |
|---|---|
| Waterwheel | Power is 20% short and there is flowing water in the domain |
| Boiler and engine | Power is 30% short and the colony stores fuel |
| Span upgrade | A rope span runs hot or snapped: its pulleys become poles and the rope becomes copper |
| Lightning mast | Lightning struck twice this week, or a surge destroyed a machine |
| Lightning arresters | After a surge: an arrester on every pole of that network |
| Pressure valve | A boiler ran high today, or a boiler burst |

Porters build only T1 projects. Until the Lineman and the Builder Drone arrive (Tier 3), you build the T2 projects:
the ghost shows every block and span, and the colony tracks your progress.
