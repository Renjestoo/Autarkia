# Steel Nugget

<ItemImage id="autarkia:steel_nugget" scale="2" />

The steel nugget is a small piece of steel. It is the ammunition of the steam repeater. Nine nuggets make one steel
ingot.

## Get it

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:steel_nugget" />

A Tier 3 <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_rack" /> drops 2 steel nuggets when it collapses.

## Use it

1. Carry steel nuggets in your inventory or in your off hand.
2. Hold the <ItemLink id="autarkia:steam_repeater" /> and fire. Each shot uses 1 steel nugget.
3. Or craft nine nuggets back into one <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_ingot" />.
