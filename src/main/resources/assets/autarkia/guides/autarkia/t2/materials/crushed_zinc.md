# Crushed Zinc Ore

<ItemImage id="autarkia:crushed_zinc" scale="2" />

Crushed zinc ore is raw zinc that a grinder broke into pieces. One raw zinc gives 1.5 crushed zinc on average, so it
gives more zinc than raw zinc in a furnace.

## Get it

| Input | Machine | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:raw_zinc" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:quern" /> or <ItemLink id="autarkia:stamp_mill" /> | 1 crushed zinc, plus 1 more at 50% |
| 1 raw zinc | <ItemLink id="autarkia:jaw_crusher" /> (Tier 3) | 2 crushed zinc |

## Use it

| Input | Machine | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 crushed zinc | <ItemLink id="autarkia:sluice" /> (100 ticks) | 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:washed_zinc" />, plus 1 copper nugget at 10% |
| 1 crushed zinc | Furnace (200 ticks) or blast furnace (100 ticks) | 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:zinc_ingot" /> |

Wash it for the best yield: 3 washed zinc make 4 zinc ingots in the <ItemLink id="autarkia:blast_smelter" />.

In Tier 4 the <ItemLink id="autarkia:froth_flotation_plant" /> turns 1 crushed zinc into 2 zinc concentrate.
