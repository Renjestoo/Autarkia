# Catwalk Stairs

<ItemImage id="autarkia:catwalk_stairs" scale="2" />

Catwalk stairs are stairs made of the steel grate of the <ItemLink id="autarkia:catwalk" />. They connect catwalks on
different levels. They work like normal stairs.

## Get it

One craft gives 4 stairs.

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:catwalk_stairs" />

## Use it

1. Place the stairs like normal stairs. They face the way you look.
2. Put a catwalk at the top and at the bottom of the stairs.

## Numbers

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Hardness | 2 |
| Blast resistance | 6 |
