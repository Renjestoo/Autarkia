# Steel Ingot

<ItemImage id="autarkia:steel_ingot" scale="2" />

Steel is iron with carbon from coal. It is the strong metal of Tier 2. Steel makes plates, gears, tools, armor and many
Tier 3 machines.

## Get it

| Inputs | Machine | Time | Outputs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 iron ingot and 2 coal or charcoal | <ItemLink id="autarkia:blast_smelter" /> (64 FE/t) | 200 ticks (10 s) | 1 steel ingot, plus 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:slag" /> at 25% |

1. Heat the blast smelter: it must reach 1,000 heat. Read the [Blast Smelter](../processing/blast_smelter.md) page.
2. Put iron ingots in one input slot.
3. Put coal or charcoal in a second input slot. Keep the third input slot empty.
4. Take the steel ingots and the slag from the output slots.

You can also craft 9 <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_nugget" /> into 1 ingot, or 1
<ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_block" /> into 9 ingots.

## Use it

- Press it into a <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_plate" /> in the <ItemLink id="autarkia:plate_press" />.
- Craft the steel tools: <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_pickaxe" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_axe" />,
  <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_shovel" /> and <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_sword" />.
- Craft the <ItemLink id="autarkia:wire_cutter" />.

## Numbers

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Energy per ingot | 12,800 FE (200 ticks at 64 FE/t, smelter already hot) |
| Coal per ingot | 2 |
