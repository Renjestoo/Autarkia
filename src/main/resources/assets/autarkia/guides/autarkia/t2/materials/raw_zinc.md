# Raw Zinc

<ItemImage id="autarkia:raw_zinc" scale="2" />

Raw zinc is the drop of zinc ore. Smelt it for zinc, or crush and wash it first for more zinc.

## Get it

- Mine <ItemLink id="autarkia:zinc_ore" /> or <ItemLink id="autarkia:deepslate_zinc_ore" /> with a stone pickaxe or
  better. Fortune gives more.
- Break an Ore Outcrop of zinc on the surface: 1 raw zinc.
- Unpack a <ItemLink id="autarkia:raw_zinc_block" />:

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:raw_zinc" />

## Use it

Pick one path. The later paths give more zinc.

| Path | Machines | Zinc for each raw zinc |
|---|---|---|
| Smelt | Furnace (200 ticks) or blast furnace (100 ticks) | 1 ingot |
| Crush, then smelt | <ItemLink id="autarkia:quern" /> or <ItemLink id="autarkia:stamp_mill" />, then furnace | 1.5 ingots on average |
| Crush, wash, blast smelt | Quern or stamp mill, <ItemLink id="autarkia:sluice" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:blast_smelter" /> | 2 ingots on average |

1. Crush raw zinc in a quern or stamp mill. You get 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:crushed_zinc" />, plus 1 more at 50%.
2. Wash the crushed zinc in the sluice. You get <ItemLink id="autarkia:washed_zinc" />.
3. Smelt 3 washed zinc in the blast smelter. You get 4 <ItemLink id="autarkia:zinc_ingot" />.
