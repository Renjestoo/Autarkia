# Steel Work Rig

Steel tools mine what diamond tools mine and last 1,200 uses. Steel armor gives 17 armor and 4 toughness, and each
piece mines 4% faster.

The <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_sledgehammer" /> has two modes on the mode key (V): 1x1 and 3x3. In 3x3 it breaks
the square around the block you hit, at right angles to the face you hit, at 35% speed.

The <ItemLink id="autarkia:steam_repeater" /> runs on pressure. Sneak and hold use to pump it to 100. Hold use to fire
a steel nugget every quarter second for 10 pressure: ten shots from a full charge. The gauge next to the hotbar shows
the pressure. It leaks slowly when you do not use it.

The <ItemLink id="autarkia:wire_cutter" /> takes a span down: click both ends and the cable comes back.
