# Steel Work Rig

Steel from the <ItemLink id="autarkia:blast_smelter" /> makes the Tier 2 tools, armor and weapon. Steel tools mine
every block that diamond tools mine and last 1,200 uses. The tools need steel ingots. The armor, the sledgehammer and
the repeater need <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_plate" /> from the <ItemLink id="autarkia:plate_press" />.

## Tools

- <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_pickaxe" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_axe" />,
  <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_shovel" /> and <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_sword" />: mining speed 7.5, 1,200 uses.
- <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_sledgehammer" />: two modes on the mode key (V by default). In 3x3 mode it breaks a
  square of 9 blocks at 35% speed.
- <ItemLink id="autarkia:wire_cutter" />: removes one span of a power network and gives back its cable.

## Armor

The steel work rig has four pieces: <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_helmet" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_chest" />,
<ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_legs" /> and <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_boots" />. The full set gives 17 armor and 4
toughness. Each piece also gives +4% block break speed, so the full set mines 16% faster.

## Weapon

The <ItemLink id="autarkia:steam_repeater" /> fires <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_nugget" /> with hand-pumped
pressure: 10 shots of 5 damage from a full charge.
