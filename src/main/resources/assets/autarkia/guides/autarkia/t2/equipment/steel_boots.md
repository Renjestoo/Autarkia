# Steel Boots

<ItemImage id="autarkia:steel_boots" scale="2" />

The steel boots is one piece of the steel work rig, the Tier 2 armor. Each piece also makes you mine 4% faster.

## Get it

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:steel_boots" />

Make the steel plates in the <ItemLink id="autarkia:plate_press" />.

## Use it

1. Put it on in the feet armor slot.
2. Wear all four pieces for 17 armor, 4 toughness and +16% block break speed.
3. Repair it with steel plates in an anvil.

| Property | Value |

| Armor | 2 |
