# Zinc Ore

<ItemImage id="autarkia:zinc_ore" scale="2" />

Zinc ore is the ore of the Tier 2 metal. Zinc and copper make brass, and brass makes the first Tier 2 machines.

## Get it

Zinc ore generates in stone in every Overworld biome.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Small veins | 8 tries per chunk, Y 0 to 64, vein size 8 |
| Large deposits | 1 chunk in 8, Y 8 to 48, vein size 18 |
| Biomes | All Overworld biomes |

A large deposit can leave an Ore Outcrop (a small rock pile) on the surface above it. It does this when it placed at
least 12 ore blocks and the surface is at most 128 blocks above it. Breaking the outcrop gives 1 raw ore.

Where a vein grows into deepslate, it places <ItemLink id="autarkia:deepslate_zinc_ore" /> instead.

## Use it

1. Mine it with a stone pickaxe or better.
2. Collect the <ItemLink id="autarkia:raw_zinc" />. Fortune gives more raw zinc. Silk Touch gives the ore block.
3. Smelt the raw zinc, or crush it for more zinc. Read [Zinc, Brass and Steel](../materials.md).

You can also smelt the ore block itself into 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:zinc_ingot" />.

## Numbers

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Hardness | 3 |
| Tool | Pickaxe, stone or better |
| Drop | 1 raw zinc (Fortune adds more) |
