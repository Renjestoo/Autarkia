# Washed Copper Ore

<ItemImage id="autarkia:washed_copper" scale="2" />

Washed copper ore is crushed copper ore that the sluice cleaned with water. In the blast smelter, 3 washed ore
make 4 ingots. That is the best Tier 2 yield.

## Get it

| Input | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:crushed_copper" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:sluice" /> | 100 ticks with flowing water | 1 washed copper ore, plus 1 gold nugget at 10% |

Crush raw copper in a <ItemLink id="autarkia:quern" /> or a <ItemLink id="autarkia:stamp_mill" /> first.

## Use it

| Input | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 washed copper ore | <ItemLink id="autarkia:blast_smelter" /> | 200 ticks (10 s) | 4 copper ingots, plus 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:slag" /> at 30% |
| 1 washed copper ore | Furnace or blast furnace | 200 or 100 ticks | 1 copper ingot |
| 2 washed copper ore | <ItemLink id="autarkia:magnetic_separator" /> (Tier 3) | 120 ticks | 3 pure copper ore, 1 slag, plus 1 gold nugget at 50% |

1. Put the washed ore in one input slot of the blast smelter. Keep the other input slots empty.
2. Wait until the smelter is hot and works.
3. Take the ingots and the slag from the output slots.
