# Steel Gear

<ItemImage id="autarkia:steel_gear" scale="2" />

The steel gear is the moving part of Tier 2 machines. Belts, engines, the waterwheel and the Hauler Crab need it.

## Get it

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:steel_gear" />

One craft uses 4 <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_plate" /> and 1 iron ingot and gives 2 steel gears.

## Use it

1. Press steel plates in the <ItemLink id="autarkia:plate_press" />.
2. Craft 4 steel plates around 1 iron ingot.
3. Use the gears in the recipes below.
