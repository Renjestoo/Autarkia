# Steel Plate

<ItemImage id="autarkia:steel_plate" scale="2" />

The steel plate is a flat sheet of steel. It is the most used part from Tier 2 on: gears, armor, boilers, engines and
most Tier 3 machines need it.

## Get it

| Input | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_ingot" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:plate_press" /> (48 FE/t) | 80 ticks (4 s) | 1 steel plate |

## Use it

1. Put steel ingots in the plate press.
2. Take the steel plates from the output slot.
3. Use the plates in the crafting recipes below. Steel plates also repair steel armor, the steel sledgehammer and the
   steam repeater in an anvil.
