# Hauler Crab

The Hauler Crab is a T2 spring worker with four cargo slots. It carries items for the colony and cleans up: snapped
cable ends, falling crates and spilled items in the domain are its debris. It collects them and puts them into the
nearest storage.

Build it in the <ItemLink id="autarkia:worker_assembler" /> from a <ItemLink id="autarkia:crab_frame" />, 3
mainsprings, 4 steel gears, 2 copper coils and 4 brass plates (900 ticks at 40 FE/t). It winds at a winding post like
the Porter. Repair it with brass plates.
