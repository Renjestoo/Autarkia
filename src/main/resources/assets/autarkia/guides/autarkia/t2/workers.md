# Hauler Crab

The Hauler Crab is the worker of Tier 2. It runs on a wound spring, like the Porter. It carries 4 stacks and keeps the
domain clean.

- It fetches, carries and delivers items for the colony.
- It collects debris: snapped cable ends, falling crates and the items that spill from them. It puts them into the
  colony storage.
- It places no blocks. Porters and you build.

You build a Hauler Crab in the <ItemLink id="autarkia:worker_assembler" />. Its frame is the
<ItemLink id="autarkia:crab_frame" />.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Spring | 12,000 T |
| Spring use | 1 T/s idle, 24 T/s moving, 12 T/s more while it carries |
| Collecting one piece of debris | 30 T |
| Cargo | 4 stacks |
| Health | 30 |
| Armor | 4 |
| Repair item | <ItemLink id="autarkia:brass_plate" /> (6 health each) |
| Cap cost | 1 |
| Most crabs at Tier 2 | 4 |

T is tension, the energy unit of a spring. A <ItemLink id="autarkia:winding_post" /> winds the crab. You can also use
the crab with an empty hand to wind it by 100 T.
