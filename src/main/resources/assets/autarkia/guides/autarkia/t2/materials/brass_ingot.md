# Brass Ingot

<ItemImage id="autarkia:brass_ingot" scale="2" />

Brass is an alloy of copper and zinc. It is the key material of early Tier 2: the plate press and the wire drawer
need brass ingots, and many machines need brass plates.

## Get it

| Inputs | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 copper ingots and 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:zinc_ingot" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:crucible" /> | 300 ticks at speed 1.0 | 4 brass ingots |
| 3 copper ingots and 1 zinc ingot | <ItemLink id="autarkia:blast_smelter" /> | 300 ticks (15 s) | 4 brass ingots |

The crucible speed depends on its heat source: a lit campfire 0.5x, a lit furnace 1.0x, lava 1.5x.

You can also craft 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:brass_block" /> into 9 brass ingots.

## Use it

1. Put the copper ingots and the zinc ingot in the crucible or the blast smelter.
2. Take the 4 brass ingots.
3. Build the <ItemLink id="autarkia:plate_press" /> and the <ItemLink id="autarkia:wire_drawer" /> with brass ingots.
4. Press the other brass ingots into <ItemLink id="autarkia:brass_plate" />.
