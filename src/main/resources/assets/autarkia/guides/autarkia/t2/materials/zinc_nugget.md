# Zinc Nugget

<ItemImage id="autarkia:zinc_nugget" scale="2" />

The zinc nugget is a small piece of zinc. Nine nuggets make one zinc ingot. Washing iron ore gives zinc nuggets as a
free extra.

## Get it

- Wash <ItemLink id="autarkia:crushed_iron" /> in the <ItemLink id="autarkia:sluice" />: 1 zinc nugget at 10% for each
  crushed iron.
- In Tier 3 the <ItemLink id="autarkia:magnetic_separator" /> gives 1 zinc nugget at 50% when it separates 2 washed
  iron.
- Craft 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:zinc_ingot" /> into 9 nuggets:

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:zinc_nugget" />

## Use it

1. Collect nine zinc nuggets.
2. Craft them into one <ItemLink id="autarkia:zinc_ingot" />.
