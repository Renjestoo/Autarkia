# Steel Helmet

<ItemImage id="autarkia:steel_helmet" scale="2" />

The steel helmet is one piece of the steel work rig, the Tier 2 armor. Each piece also makes you mine 4% faster.

## Get it

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:steel_helmet" />

Make the steel plates in the <ItemLink id="autarkia:plate_press" />.

## Use it

1. Put it on in the head armor slot.
2. Wear all four pieces for 17 armor, 4 toughness and +16% block break speed.
3. Repair it with steel plates in an anvil.

| Armor | 3 |
