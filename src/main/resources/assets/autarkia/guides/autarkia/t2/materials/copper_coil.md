# Copper Coil

<ItemImage id="autarkia:copper_coil" scale="2" />

The copper coil is copper wire wound on an iron core. Generators, motors and electric machines need it.

## Get it

| Inputs | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 copper ingots and 1 iron ingot (the core) | <ItemLink id="autarkia:wire_drawer" /> (40 FE/t) | 100 ticks (5 s) | 2 copper coils |

## Use it

1. Put copper ingots in one input slot of the wire drawer.
2. Put iron ingots in the other input slot.
3. Take the copper coils from the output slot.
