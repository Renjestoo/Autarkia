# Washed Zinc Ore

<ItemImage id="autarkia:washed_zinc" scale="2" />

Washed zinc ore is crushed zinc ore that the sluice cleaned with water. In the blast smelter, 3 washed ore
make 4 ingots. That is the best Tier 2 yield.

## Get it

| Input | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:crushed_zinc" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:sluice" /> | 100 ticks with flowing water | 1 washed zinc ore, plus 1 copper nugget at 10% |

Crush raw zinc in a <ItemLink id="autarkia:quern" /> or a <ItemLink id="autarkia:stamp_mill" /> first.

## Use it

| Input | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 washed zinc ore | <ItemLink id="autarkia:blast_smelter" /> | 200 ticks (10 s) | 4 <ItemLink id="autarkia:zinc_ingot" />, plus 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:slag" /> at 30% |
| 1 washed zinc ore | Furnace or blast furnace | 200 or 100 ticks | 1 zinc ingot |
| 2 washed zinc ore | <ItemLink id="autarkia:magnetic_separator" /> (Tier 3) | 120 ticks | 3 pure zinc ore, 1 slag, plus 1 copper nugget at 50% |

1. Put the washed ore in one input slot of the blast smelter. Keep the other input slots empty.
2. Wait until the smelter is hot and works.
3. Take the ingots and the slag from the output slots.
