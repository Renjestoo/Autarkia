# Steel Axe

<ItemImage id="autarkia:steel_axe" scale="2" />

The steel axe cuts wood fast and hits hard. A hit on a raised shield disables the shield for 5 seconds.

## Get it

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:steel_axe" />

Make the steel ingots in the <ItemLink id="autarkia:blast_smelter" />.

## Use it

1. Hold the axe.
2. Cut logs and other wood blocks.
3. Use it on a log to strip the bark.
4. Repair it with steel ingots in an anvil.
