# Steel Sword

<ItemImage id="autarkia:steel_sword" scale="2" />

The steel sword is the Tier 2 melee weapon. It hits harder than an iron sword and lasts longer.

## Get it

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:steel_sword" />

Make the steel ingots in the <ItemLink id="autarkia:blast_smelter" />.

## Use it

1. Hold the sword.
2. Attack at full charge for the sweep attack.
3. Repair it with steel ingots in an anvil.

## Numbers

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Attack damage | 6.5 |
| Attack speed | 1.6 |
| Durability | 1,200 uses |
| Durability per hit on a mob | 1 |
| Enchantability | 12 |
| Repair | Steel ingot |
