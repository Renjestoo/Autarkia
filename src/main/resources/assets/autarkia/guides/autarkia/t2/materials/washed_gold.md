# Washed Gold Ore

<ItemImage id="autarkia:washed_gold" scale="2" />

Washed gold ore is crushed gold ore that the sluice cleaned with water. In the blast smelter, 3 washed ore
make 4 ingots. That is the best Tier 2 yield.

## Get it

| Input | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:crushed_gold" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:sluice" /> | 100 ticks with flowing water | 1 washed gold ore, plus 1 copper nugget at 10% |

Crush raw gold in a <ItemLink id="autarkia:quern" /> or a <ItemLink id="autarkia:stamp_mill" /> first.

## Use it

| Input | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 washed gold ore | <ItemLink id="autarkia:blast_smelter" /> | 200 ticks (10 s) | 4 gold ingots, plus 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:slag" /> at 30% |
| 1 washed gold ore | Furnace or blast furnace | 200 or 100 ticks | 1 gold ingot |
| 2 washed gold ore | <ItemLink id="autarkia:magnetic_separator" /> (Tier 3) | 120 ticks | 3 pure gold ore, 1 slag, plus 1 copper nugget at 50% |

1. Put the washed ore in one input slot of the blast smelter. Keep the other input slots empty.
2. Wait until the smelter is hot and works.
3. Take the ingots and the slag from the output slots.
