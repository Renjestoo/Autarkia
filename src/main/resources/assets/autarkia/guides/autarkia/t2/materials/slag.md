# Slag

<ItemImage id="autarkia:slag" scale="2" />

Slag is the waste of hot ore processing. Do not throw it away: 4 slag make 2 foundation, the base block of every
heavy machine.

## Get it

| Recipe | Machine | Slag |
|---|---|---|
| 3 washed ore to 4 ingots | <ItemLink id="autarkia:blast_smelter" /> | 1 at 30% |
| 1 iron ingot and 2 coal to 1 steel ingot | Blast smelter | 1 at 25% |
| 2 washed ore to 3 pure ore | <ItemLink id="autarkia:magnetic_separator" /> (Tier 3) | 1 every time |
| 1 crushed ore to 2 concentrate | <ItemLink id="autarkia:froth_flotation_plant" /> (Tier 4) | 1 at 50% |

## Use it

1. Collect 4 slag.
2. Put them in a 2 x 2 square in a crafting grid.
3. Take 2 <ItemLink id="autarkia:foundation" />.
