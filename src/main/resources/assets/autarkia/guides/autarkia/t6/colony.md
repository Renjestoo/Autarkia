# Seeds, Restoration and Bastion

These blocks make your colony grow, repair itself and resist attacks.

- The <ItemLink id="autarkia:core_upgrade_t6" /> raises the Colony Core to tier 6.
- The <ItemLink id="autarkia:restoration_projector" /> takes a snapshot of an area and puts back the blocks that you
  lose.
- The <ItemLink id="autarkia:seed_matrix" /> packs up to 8 recorded Patterns into one
  <ItemLink id="autarkia:autarky_seed" />. You throw the seed, and a complete outpost base rises from the ground.
- <ItemLink id="autarkia:bastion_block" /> and <ItemLink id="autarkia:bastion_glass" /> cannot break while your domain
  grid has an ONLINE Singularity Core and 1,000,000 FE.
