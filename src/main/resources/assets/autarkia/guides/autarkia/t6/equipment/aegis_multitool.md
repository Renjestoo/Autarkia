# Aegis Multitool

<ItemImage id="autarkia:aegis_multitool" scale="2" />

The Aegis Multitool is every tool in one: pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe, shears and sword. It mines at netherite level with
speed 2,000, so it breaks blocks up to hardness 66 at once. It is unbreakable and charges by itself from your domain
grid.

## Get it

There is no crafting recipe. The <ItemLink id="autarkia:fabricator" /> makes it:

| Inputs | Machine | Time | FE | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:nanite_multitool" />, 4 <ItemLink id="autarkia:aegis_plate" />, 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:ai_core" />, 4 <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_crystal" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:fabricator" /> | 1,200 ticks | 8,334 FE/t | 1 Aegis Multitool |

## Use it

1. Hold it and mine any block. Each block costs 50 FE.
2. Press the mode key (V by default) to change the area: 1x1, 3x3 or 5x5. The area modes mine at full speed.
3. Press the enchant key (B by default) to change the enchantment: none, Silk Touch or Fortune III.
4. Use it on a block to strip logs, make paths or till soil, like the matching tool.
5. It also does the actions of shears.

The tooltip shows the enchant mode, for example "Enchant mode: Silk".

## Charging

- Keep it anywhere in your inventory within 64 blocks of a tether point. The grid charges it at up to 50,000 FE/t.
- A tether point is a Colony Core, an outpost core, a domain beacon or a phase node on a grid with an ONLINE
  Singularity Core.
- An external charger also works.

## Problems

- It mines slowly and drops nothing: it has 0 FE. Go near a tether point, or charge it.
- At 0 FE it does not strip, till or shear.
- The mode message shows "Mode: 1x1", "Mode: 3x3" or "Mode: 5x5". The enchant message shows "Enchant: No enchant",
  "Enchant: Silk" or "Enchant: Fortune".
