# Tier 6: Ascendant

Tier 6 writes matter. The Singularity Core powers the domain and drinks explosions. The Gyro Heart stores the energy,
phase links carry it without cables, and the matter compiler writes any metal from matter units. The domain archive
and matter conduits move items at once. The Seed Matrix packs your Patterns into an Autarky Seed that unpacks a whole
base. The Restoration Field repairs damage by itself, Bastion blocks cannot break, and Aegis gear makes you
practically indestructible while the grid holds. Use a <ItemLink id="autarkia:core_upgrade_t6" /> on your Colony Core
to start it.

## Watch for

- You throw the Autarky Seed. Light runs over the ground, blocks rise from the soil, and a powered base with drones
  stands there after about one minute. See the colony page.
- Ceiling arms in the Forge of Ascension fit your Aegis armor plate by plate, with sparks and camera shake. See the
  equipment page.
- A Wither explodes against your walls. No block breaks, and every lamp in the domain flashes as the Singularity Core
  drinks the blast. See the power page.
- A lethal hit turns your body into a nanite cloud that forms again 3 blocks away. See the equipment page.
- A creeper crater fills again by itself: debris flies back on reverse arcs and the blocks return in a wave from the
  edge. See the colony page.
