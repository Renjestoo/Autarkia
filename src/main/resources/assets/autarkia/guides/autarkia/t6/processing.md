# The Matter Compiler

Tier 6 processing writes items from matter. MU (matter units) is the free matter that your Matter Buffers hold.

- The <ItemLink id="autarkia:matter_compiler" /> changes MU into metal ingots, nuggets, lattice crystals, lattice alloy
  and aegis plates.
- The <ItemLink id="autarkia:aegis_plate" /> is the main material of tier 6. Only the matter compiler makes it. Almost
  every tier 6 block and all Aegis gear need it.

Feed the compiler raw ore: in the compiler one raw ore gives the MU of 8 of its ingots.
