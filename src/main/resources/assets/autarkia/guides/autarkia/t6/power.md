# Singularity and Phase Links

Tier 6 power has three parts. They work together as one domain grid.

- The <ItemLink id="autarkia:singularity_core" /> is the generator. After one ignition it makes 131,072 FE/t. It also
  changes every explosion in your domain into FE.
- The <ItemLink id="autarkia:gyro_heart" /> is the storage. It holds 2,000,000,000 FE.
- The <ItemLink id="autarkia:phase_node" /> carries the power without a cable. A link goes up to 64 blocks through the
  air.

The domain grid is every network that has a node inside your domain and an ONLINE Singularity Core. This grid pays for
the Aegis armor, it powers the Bastion blocks, and it charges your Aegis tools.
