# Bastion Glass

<ItemImage id="autarkia:bastion_glass" scale="2" />

Bastion glass is the clear form of the <ItemLink id="autarkia:bastion_block" />. It has the same protection rules. Use
it for windows in your Bastion walls.

## Get it

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:bastion_glass" />

8 <ItemLink id="minecraft:glass" /> around 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:aegis_plate" /> give 8 Bastion glass.

## Use it

1. Put the glass into your walls inside your colony domain.
2. Keep an ONLINE <ItemLink id="autarkia:singularity_core" /> and at least 1,000,000 FE on the grid of the domain.
3. Look at the glass with Jade. "Protected" means it cannot break.

Mobs cannot spawn on Bastion glass. It does not carry redstone power and it does not suffocate you.

## Numbers

| Property | Powered | Unpowered |
|---|---|---|
| Break by a survival player | no progress | like obsidian |
| Blast resistance | 3,600,000 | 1,200 |
| Wither, Ender Dragon, other mobs | cannot break it | normal Minecraft rules |
| Pistons | cannot move it | cannot move it |
| Hardness | 50 | 50 |
| Mining tool | Diamond pickaxe or better | Diamond pickaxe or better |

## Jade

- "Protected (grid N FE)": the glass is powered.
- "Unprotected": the glass acts like obsidian.

## Problems

- "Unprotected": the glass is outside your domain, no Singularity Core is ONLINE, or the grid holds less than
  1,000,000 FE.
