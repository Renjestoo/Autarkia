# Archive and Conduits

In tier 6, items move at once, with no carrier. Both blocks work only inside your colony domain, and both use FE.

- A <ItemLink id="autarkia:matter_conduit" /> in SEND mode sends its items to the conduits in RECEIVE mode on the same
  color channel. Up to 64 items move each tick.
- The <ItemLink id="autarkia:domain_archive" /> shows the stock of all storage in the domain. A request moves up to
  1,728 items at once into its buffer or into a receiving conduit.

Use the archive to find and fetch items. Use conduits for fixed item lines between machines and storage.
