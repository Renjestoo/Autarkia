# Aegis Boots

<ItemImage id="autarkia:aegis_boots" scale="2" />

The Aegis Boots are the foot piece of the Aegis armor. They are unbreakable and hold 10,000,000 FE. They keep every
function of the Exo Rig, Frontier Suit and Lattice Carapace boots: the jet and the fall protection. With all 4 Aegis
pieces on, your domain grid pays your damage and a lethal hit cannot kill you.

## Get it

You cannot craft them. The <ItemLink id="autarkia:forge_of_ascension" /> fits them on your body. The boots are the
first piece of a fitting:

| Inputs | Worn piece | Machine | Time | FE | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 <ItemLink id="autarkia:aegis_plate" />, 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:ai_core" />, 4 <ItemLink id="autarkia:nanite_paste" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_carapace_boots" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:forge_of_ascension" /> | ticks 60 to 160 of the fitting | 12,500,000 | 1 Aegis Boots |

## Use it

1. Wear them. The functions work while the suit is on and the piece has FE.
2. Jump, then hold the jump key in the air. The jet lifts you.
3. Press the suit key (N by default) to turn all your suit pieces on or off.
4. Stay within 64 blocks of a tether point. Then the functions cost no FE and the piece charges by itself.

A tether point is a Colony Core, an outpost core, a domain beacon or a phase node on a grid with an ONLINE Singularity
Core.

## Functions

- Jet: while you hold jump in the air, your upward speed grows by 0.10 per tick up to 0.5. You also steer better in the
  air. Without the tether the jet costs 80 FE/t. A jet tick resets your fall distance.
- Fall protection: the boots cancel the fall damage above 3 blocks. While you are tethered this costs nothing. Without
  the tether it costs 50 FE per block above 3.

The jet does not work in water, while you fly, while you glide with an elytra or while you ride.

## Charging

- While you are tethered, the grid charges every Aegis item in your inventory at up to 50,000 FE/t.
- An external charger puts in at most 50,000 FE/t.

## The full set

With all 4 Aegis pieces on (<ItemLink id="autarkia:aegis_helmet" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:aegis_chest" />,
<ItemLink id="autarkia:aegis_legs" /> and <ItemLink id="autarkia:aegis_boots" />):

- Aegis rule: while you are tethered, each damage point (before armor) costs the grid 50,000 FE instead of your health.
  The grid pays all or nothing. A paid hit gives no damage and no knockback.
- Nanite Reform: a lethal hit costs 2,000 free MU instead of your life. You form again 3 blocks away with half health.
  The cooldown is 30 seconds.
- The HUD shows "Tether" (green, amber or red) and "Reform ready" or "Reform N s".

The <ItemLink id="autarkia:aegis_chest" /> page explains all rules of the full set.

## Problems

- "No suit pieces worn": you pressed the suit key without suit armor on.
- "Suit off": the functions stop. Press the suit key again.
- The jet does not fire: the suit is off, or the boots have less than 80 FE.
