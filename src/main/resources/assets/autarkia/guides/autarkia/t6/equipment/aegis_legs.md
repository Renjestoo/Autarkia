# Aegis Leggings

<ItemImage id="autarkia:aegis_legs" scale="2" />

The Aegis Leggings are the leg piece of the Aegis armor. They are unbreakable and hold 10,000,000 FE. They keep every
function of the Exo Rig, Frontier Suit and Lattice Carapace leggings. With all 4 Aegis pieces on, your domain grid pays
your damage and a lethal hit cannot kill you.

## Get it

You cannot craft them. The <ItemLink id="autarkia:forge_of_ascension" /> fits them on your body:

| Inputs | Worn piece | Machine | Time | FE | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 <ItemLink id="autarkia:aegis_plate" />, 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:ai_core" />, 6 <ItemLink id="autarkia:nanite_paste" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_carapace_legs" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:forge_of_ascension" /> | ticks 160 to 280 of the fitting | 12,500,000 | 1 Aegis Leggings |

## Use it

1. Wear them. The functions work while the suit is on and the piece has FE.
2. Press the suit key (N by default) to turn all your suit pieces on or off.
3. Stay within 64 blocks of a tether point. Then the functions cost no FE and the piece charges by itself.

A tether point is a Colony Core, an outpost core, a domain beacon or a phase node on a grid with an ONLINE Singularity
Core.

## Functions

- +0.5 step height: you walk up one more half block. Without the tether it costs 2 FE per tick while you move on the
  ground.
- +20% speed while you sprint. Without the tether it costs 10 FE/t while you sprint.

## Charging

- While you are tethered, the grid charges every Aegis item in your inventory at up to 50,000 FE/t.
- An external charger puts in at most 50,000 FE/t.

## The full set

With all 4 Aegis pieces on (<ItemLink id="autarkia:aegis_helmet" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:aegis_chest" />,
<ItemLink id="autarkia:aegis_legs" /> and <ItemLink id="autarkia:aegis_boots" />):

- Aegis rule: while you are tethered, each damage point (before armor) costs the grid 50,000 FE instead of your health.
  The grid pays all or nothing. A paid hit gives no damage and no knockback.
- Nanite Reform: a lethal hit costs 2,000 free MU instead of your life. You form again 3 blocks away with half health.
  The cooldown is 30 seconds.
- The HUD shows "Tether" (green, amber or red) and "Reform ready" or "Reform N s".

The <ItemLink id="autarkia:aegis_chest" /> page explains all rules of the full set.

## Problems

- "No suit pieces worn": you pressed the suit key without suit armor on.
- "Suit off": the functions stop. Press the suit key again.
