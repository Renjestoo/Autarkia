# Aegis Plate

<ItemImage id="autarkia:aegis_plate" scale="2" />

The aegis plate is the main material of tier 6. Only the <ItemLink id="autarkia:matter_compiler" /> makes it. You need
it for the Core Upgrade to tier 6, for almost every tier 6 block and for all Aegis gear.

## Get it

There is no crafting recipe. The matter compiler makes it:

| Input | Machine | Time | FE | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 32,768 MU | <ItemLink id="autarkia:matter_compiler" /> | 1,024 ticks (51.2 s) | 16,384 FE/t, 16,777,216 FE in total | 1 aegis plate |

1. Set the aegis plate as the target of the compiler.
2. Give the compiler 32,768 MU from its feed slot or from the Matter Buffers of your domain.
3. Take the plate from an output slot.

## Numbers

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Matter value | 32,768 MU |
| Made by | Matter compiler only |
