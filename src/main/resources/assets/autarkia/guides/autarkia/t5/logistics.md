# Matter and Mag Rail

Tier 5 brings matter units (MU). Every item has a matter value. The <ItemLink id="autarkia:matter_buffer" /> stores
items by their MU, and it holds free matter: matter that is no longer an item. Nanite claims fill the buffers, linked
tools stream their drops into them, and the Replicator Foundry takes the matter for each outpost kit from them.

The mag rail moves crates fast over long distances. Lay <ItemLink id="autarkia:mag_rail" /> between two or more
<ItemLink id="autarkia:mag_rail_station" /> blocks. A station packs 27 stacks into one crate and sends it as a
floating sled at up to 20 blocks per second. One track carries sleds in both directions.

The <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_rack" /> holds 4 crates with a load rating of 65,536 units, so it carries even 4
crates of iron blocks.
