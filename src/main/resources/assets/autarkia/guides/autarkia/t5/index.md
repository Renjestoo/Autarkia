# Tier 5: Matter

Tier 5 takes matter apart. It brings lattice crystals from deep geodes, the plasma separator (6 ingots from 1 raw
ore), the plasma generator, superconductor lines that must stay cold, the Matter Buffer, mag rails and lattice racks.
The Nanite Hive takes whole hills apart, and the Replicator Foundry prints walkers that found new outposts by
themselves. Use a <ItemLink id="autarkia:core_upgrade_t5" /> on your Colony Core to start it.

## Watch for

- You mark a hill. A nanite cloud takes it apart layer by layer. Ores fly out as sorted colour streams, a buried chest
  stays in a floating bubble, and you get a clean terraced pit. See the colony page.
- The Replicator Foundry prints a walker. It unfolds its legs, walks to a deposit and digs in. At dusk a lamp-lit
  cable line crosses the land to a base you did not build. See the colony page.
- A superconductor warms up. Frost turns to steam and the line quenches with a blast. See the power page.
