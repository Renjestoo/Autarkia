# Lattice Alloy Ingot

<ItemImage id="autarkia:lattice_alloy_ingot" scale="2" />

Lattice alloy is the metal of Tier 5. It goes into the Core Upgrade T5, the Tier 5 machines, the Replicator Walker and
the lattice equipment.

## Get it

The <ItemLink id="autarkia:plasma_separator" /> alloys it. Machine recipes do not show in this book, so the table
gives the recipe.

| Inputs | Machine | Time | Energy | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_crystal" />, 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_steel_ingot" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:plasma_separator" /> | 200 ticks (10 s) | 4,096 FE/t | 1 lattice alloy ingot |

1. Put the lattice crystal into input A of the plasma separator.
2. Put the vantite steel ingot into input B.
3. Take the ingot from an output slot.

A <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_alloy_block" /> gives back 9 ingots in a crafting grid:

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:lattice_alloy_ingot" />

In Tier 6 the <ItemLink id="autarkia:matter_compiler" /> also writes an ingot from 1,188 MU of matter.

with it.
