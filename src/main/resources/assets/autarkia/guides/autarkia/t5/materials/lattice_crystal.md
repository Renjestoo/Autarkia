# Lattice Crystal

<ItemImage id="autarkia:lattice_crystal" scale="2" />

The lattice crystal is 4 lattice shards pressed into one. It goes into the lattice alloy, the AI core and most Tier 5
machines.

## Get it

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:lattice_crystal" />

In Tier 6 the <ItemLink id="autarkia:matter_compiler" /> also writes a crystal from 864 MU of matter.

## Use it

1. Put 4 <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_shard" /> in a 2 x 2 square in a crafting grid.
2. Take the crystal.
3. Alloy it with a vantite steel ingot in the <ItemLink id="autarkia:plasma_separator" />.

| Product | Crystals | Machine |
|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_alloy_ingot" /> | 1, plus 1 vantite steel ingot | <ItemLink id="autarkia:plasma_separator" /> |
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:ai_core" /> | 1 | <ItemLink id="autarkia:fabricator" /> |
| 4 <ItemLink id="autarkia:superconductor_spool" /> | 4 | Crafting grid |
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:plasma_separator" /> | 1 | Crafting grid |
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:matter_buffer" /> | 1 | Crafting grid |
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_rack" /> | 3 | Crafting grid |

The lattice equipment and the <ItemLink id="autarkia:core_upgrade_t6" /> (8 crystals) also use crystals.
