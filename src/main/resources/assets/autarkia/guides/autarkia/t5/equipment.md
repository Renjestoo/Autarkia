# Lattice Equipment

The fabricator makes the Tier 5 equipment from your Tier 4 equipment and lattice materials. The new item keeps the FE of
the old one. All lattice equipment uses FE and takes a charge of up to 20,000 FE/t. Charge it in the item slot of a
<ItemLink id="autarkia:battery_bank" /> or a <ItemLink id="autarkia:capacitor_bank" />.

- The <ItemLink id="autarkia:nanite_multitool" /> is pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe, shears and sword in one, with a 3 x 3
  mode. It comes from the vantite excavator.
- The <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_blade" /> hits for 12, and its powered hits halve the armor of the target. It comes
  from the vantite blade.
- The <ItemLink id="autarkia:disassembler_beam" /> takes blocks apart from up to 24 blocks away.
- The Lattice Carapace comes from the Frontier Suit: <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_carapace_helmet" />,
  <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_carapace_chest" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_carapace_legs" /> and
  <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_carapace_boots" />. It keeps every Exo Rig and Frontier Suit function and repairs itself
  with FE.

The matter link: sneak and use the multitool or the beam on a <ItemLink id="autarkia:matter_buffer" />. The tool then
turns drops into matter in its cache (up to 4,096 MU) and streams the matter to the buffer within 32 blocks.

In Tier 6 the Forge of Ascension needs the worn Lattice Carapace to fit the Aegis armor.
