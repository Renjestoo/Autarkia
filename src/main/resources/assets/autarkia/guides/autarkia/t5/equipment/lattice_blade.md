# Lattice Blade

<ItemImage id="autarkia:lattice_blade" scale="2" />

The Lattice Blade is the Tier 5 sword. Its powered hits cut through armor: the armor of the target protects only half
as much.

## Get it

The <ItemLink id="autarkia:fabricator" /> makes it from the <ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_blade" />. Fabricator
recipes do not show in this book, so the table gives the recipe.

| Inputs | Machine | Time | Energy | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_blade" />, 4 <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_alloy_ingot" />, 2 <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_crystal" />, 2 <ItemLink id="autarkia:nanite_paste" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:fabricator" /> | 600 ticks (30 s) | 1,334 FE/t | 1 Lattice Blade |

The blade keeps the FE of the vantite blade.

## Use it

1. Charge the blade. Put it into the item slot of a <ItemLink id="autarkia:battery_bank" /> (2,048 FE/t) or a
   <ItemLink id="autarkia:capacitor_bank" /> (512 FE/t).
2. Hit a target. Each hit costs 200 FE.
3. A hit with at least 200 FE in the blade halves the armor protection of the target for that hit.

## Numbers

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Attack damage | 12 |
| Attack speed | 1.6 attacks per second |
| Energy capacity | 5,000,000 FE |
| Charge rate | Up to 20,000 FE/t |
| Cost per hit | 200 FE |
| Armor effect | The armor reduction of the hit is x 0.5 |
| Durability | None: it does not break |

## Problems

- The hits do not pierce armor: the blade has less than 200 FE. It hits as a normal sword. Charge it.
