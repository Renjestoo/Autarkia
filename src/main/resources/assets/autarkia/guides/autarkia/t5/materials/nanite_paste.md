# Nanite Paste

<ItemImage id="autarkia:nanite_paste" scale="2" />

Nanite paste is a mass of tiny machines. The Nanite Hive builds swarms from it, and the AI core, the Replicator Walker
and the lattice equipment need it.

## Get it

The <ItemLink id="autarkia:fabricator" /> makes it. Fabricator recipes do not show in this book, so the table gives
the recipe.

| Inputs | Machine | Time | Energy | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_shard" />, 4 redstone dust, 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:slime_ball" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:fabricator" /> | 200 ticks (10 s) | 160 FE/t | 2 nanite paste |

1. Put the inputs into the fabricator buffer.
2. Select "Nanite Paste" as the fabricator target.
3. Take the 2 paste from the output.

A Nanite Swarm gives 2 paste back when it dissolves at its hive, and it drops 2 paste when it is destroyed.
