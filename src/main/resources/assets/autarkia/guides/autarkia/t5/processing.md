# Plasma Separation

Tier 5 processes ore in one step. The <ItemLink id="autarkia:plasma_separator" /> turns 1 raw ore into 6 ingots, with a
20% chance of 1 ingot of a second metal. It works on raw iron, copper, gold, tin, zinc and vantite. It uses 4,096 FE/t
and must stand on foundation.

The separator also alloys the <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_alloy_ingot" /> from a
<ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_crystal" /> and a vantite steel ingot. You need this alloy for the Core Upgrade T5, so
the separator is the first machine of the tier.

Raw ore from your nanite claims goes straight into the separator.
