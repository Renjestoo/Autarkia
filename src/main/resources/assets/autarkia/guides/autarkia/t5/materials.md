# Lattice Materials

All Tier 5 materials start in lattice geodes deep under the Overworld (Y -58 to Y -24). The inner wall of a geode is
made of <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_geode_shell" />. Charged shells grow <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_cluster" />
crystals, and a netherite-level pickaxe mines 2 <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_shard" /> from each cluster.

From the shards you make the rest:

- 4 shards craft a <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_crystal" />.
- The fabricator turns shards, redstone and slime into <ItemLink id="autarkia:nanite_paste" />.
- Circuits, a crystal, paste and a diamond make the <ItemLink id="autarkia:ai_core" />.
- The plasma separator alloys a crystal and a vantite steel ingot into the <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_alloy_ingot" />.
- 9 ingots make a <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_alloy_block" />. 1 ingot and 8 stone make 8
  <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_block" />.

Leave the charged shells in the geode. They are the only source of new clusters.
