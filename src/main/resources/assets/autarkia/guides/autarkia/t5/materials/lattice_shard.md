# Lattice Shard

<ItemImage id="autarkia:lattice_shard" scale="2" />

The lattice shard is the raw material of Tier 5. Every Tier 5 machine, cell and tool starts with shards.

## Get it

The shard has no crafting recipe.

- Mine a <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_cluster" /> with a netherite-level pickaxe: 2 shards, more with Fortune.
- A collapsed <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_rack" /> drops 2 shards.
