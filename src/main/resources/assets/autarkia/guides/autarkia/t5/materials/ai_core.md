# AI Core

<ItemImage id="autarkia:ai_core" scale="2" />

The AI core is the brain of the Tier 5 machines. You need one for the Core Upgrade T5, the plasma generator, the
Replicator Foundry and each Replicator Walker.

## Get it

The <ItemLink id="autarkia:fabricator" /> makes it. Fabricator recipes do not show in this book, so the table gives
the recipe.

| Inputs | Machine | Time | Energy | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 <ItemLink id="autarkia:advanced_circuit" />, 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_crystal" />, 2 <ItemLink id="autarkia:nanite_paste" />, 1 <ItemLink id="minecraft:diamond" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:fabricator" /> | 400 ticks (20 s) | 160 FE/t | 1 AI core |

1. Put the inputs into the fabricator buffer.
2. Select "AI Core" as the fabricator target.
3. Take the AI core from the output.
