# Lattice Carapace Leggings

<ItemImage id="autarkia:lattice_carapace_legs" scale="2" />

The Lattice Carapace Leggings are the leg piece of the Tier 5 powered armor. They keep the Exo Rig and Frontier Suit
leg functions, and they repair themselves with FE.

## Get it

The <ItemLink id="autarkia:fabricator" /> makes them from the <ItemLink id="autarkia:frontier_suit_legs" />. Fabricator
recipes do not show in this book, so the table gives the recipe.

| Inputs | Machine | Time | Energy | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:frontier_suit_legs" />, 7 <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_alloy_ingot" />, 3 <ItemLink id="autarkia:nanite_paste" />, 2 <ItemLink id="autarkia:lattice_crystal" />, 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:coolant_cell" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:fabricator" /> | 700 ticks (35 s) | 1,429 FE/t | 1 Lattice Carapace Leggings |

The new leggings keep the FE of the Frontier Suit leggings.

## Use it

1. Charge the leggings. Put them into the item slot of a <ItemLink id="autarkia:battery_bank" /> (2,048 FE/t) or a
   <ItemLink id="autarkia:capacitor_bank" /> (512 FE/t).
2. Wear them.
3. Press the suit key (default N) to switch the suit on or off.

The functions work while the suit is on and the leggings have FE:

- +0.5 step height, for 2 FE per tick while you move on the ground.
- +20% speed while you sprint, for 10 FE/t.
- Self-repair: while the leggings are worn and damaged, they mend 1 durability every 20 ticks for 2,000 FE.

## Problems

- No sprint boost: the suit is off, or the leggings have less than 10 FE. Press the suit key or charge them.
