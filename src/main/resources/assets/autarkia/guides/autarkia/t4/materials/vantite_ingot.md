# Vantite Ingot

<ItemImage id="autarkia:vantite_ingot" scale="2" />

The vantite ingot is the base metal of Tier 4. It goes into the advanced circuit, vantite steel and the logistics
blocks. There is no vantite nugget.

## Get it

A furnace cannot make vantite ingots. Use one of these machines:

| Input | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:raw_vantite" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:blast_smelter" /> | 200 ticks, 64 FE/t | 1 vantite ingot |
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:crushed_vantite" /> | Blast smelter | 200 ticks, 64 FE/t | 1 vantite ingot |
| 3 <ItemLink id="autarkia:washed_vantite" /> | Blast smelter | 200 ticks, 64 FE/t | 4 vantite ingots, slag (30%) |
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:pure_vantite" /> | Blast smelter | 200 ticks, 64 FE/t | 1 vantite ingot |
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_concentrate" /> | Blast smelter | 200 ticks, 64 FE/t | 1 vantite ingot |
| 1 pure vantite | <ItemLink id="autarkia:electrolyzer" /> | 160 ticks, 256 FE/t | 1 vantite ingot, gold nugget (10%) |
| 1 vantite concentrate | Electrolyzer | 160 ticks, 256 FE/t | 1 vantite ingot, gold nugget (10%) |

You can also take 9 ingots out of a <ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_block" />:

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:vantite_ingot" />

## Use it

- 1 vantite ingot and 1 steel ingot: 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_steel_ingot" /> in the blast smelter.
- 2 basic circuits and 1 vantite ingot: 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:advanced_circuit" /> in the electrolyzer.
- Crafting: <ItemLink id="autarkia:cable_car_station" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:crate_launcher" />,
  <ItemLink id="autarkia:catch_net" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:depot" />.
- Worker assembler: 2 for each Surveyor.
- 9 ingots: 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_block" />.
