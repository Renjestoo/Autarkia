# Frontier Suit Boots

<ItemImage id="autarkia:frontier_suit_boots" scale="2" />

The Frontier Suit Boots are the Tier 4 upgrade of the exo rig boots. They have jets: hold jump in the air to fly up.
They also stop fall damage for FE. They run on FE.

## Get it

| Inputs | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:exo_rig_boots" />, 4 <ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_steel_ingot" />, 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:advanced_circuit" />, 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:battery_bank" />, 2 blaze rods | <ItemLink id="autarkia:fabricator" /> | 400 ticks, 375 FE/t (150,000 FE) | 1 Frontier Suit Boots |

The FE in the old exo rig boots goes into the new boots.

## Use it

1. Charge the boots in the item slot of a <ItemLink id="autarkia:battery_bank" /> (2,048 FE/t) or a
   <ItemLink id="autarkia:capacitor_bank" /> (512 FE/t).
2. Wear them and jump.
3. Hold jump in the air. The jets add 0.10 upward speed each tick, up to 0.5 blocks per tick. They cost 80 FE/t.
4. Steer with the movement keys. The jets give you twice the normal air control.
5. Release jump to fall. A jet tick resets your fall distance.
6. Press the suit key (default N) to turn all worn suit pieces on or off.

The jets do not work in water, while you glide with an elytra, while you ride, or in creative flight. They need at
least 80 FE.

### Fall protection

When you fall more than 3 blocks, the boots cancel the damage. Each block above 3 costs 50 FE. With too little FE
they cancel only a part of the fall.

## Problems

- The jets do not fire: the boots have less than 80 FE, the suit is off, or you are in water or on a mount.
- You still take fall damage: the boots had too little FE for the whole fall.
