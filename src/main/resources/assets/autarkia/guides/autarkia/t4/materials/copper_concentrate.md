# Copper Concentrate

<ItemImage id="autarkia:copper_concentrate" scale="2" />

Copper concentrate comes from the froth flotation plant. 1 crushed copper ore gives 2 concentrate, and each
concentrate gives 1 copper ingot. With the jaw crusher this is 4 copper ingots for each raw copper.

## Get it

| Input | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:crushed_copper" /> and 250 mB water | <ItemLink id="autarkia:froth_flotation_plant" /> | 200 ticks, 768 FE/t | 2 copper concentrate, <ItemLink id="autarkia:slag" /> (50%), gold nugget (35%) |

## Use it

| Input | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 copper concentrate | Furnace | 200 ticks | 1 copper ingot |
| 1 copper concentrate | Blast furnace | 100 ticks | 1 copper ingot |
| 1 copper concentrate | <ItemLink id="autarkia:electrolyzer" /> | 160 ticks, 256 FE/t | 1 copper ingot, gold nugget (10%) |

The <ItemLink id="autarkia:blast_smelter" /> also smelts it, like a furnace.
