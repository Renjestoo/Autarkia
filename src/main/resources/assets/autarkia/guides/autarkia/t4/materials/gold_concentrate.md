# Gold Concentrate

<ItemImage id="autarkia:gold_concentrate" scale="2" />

Gold concentrate comes from the froth flotation plant. 1 crushed gold ore gives 2 concentrate, and each concentrate
gives 1 gold ingot. With the jaw crusher this is 4 gold ingots for each raw gold.

## Get it

| Input | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:crushed_gold" /> and 250 mB water | <ItemLink id="autarkia:froth_flotation_plant" /> | 200 ticks, 768 FE/t | 2 gold concentrate, <ItemLink id="autarkia:slag" /> (50%), copper nugget (35%) |

## Use it

| Input | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 gold concentrate | Furnace | 200 ticks | 1 gold ingot |
| 1 gold concentrate | Blast furnace | 100 ticks | 1 gold ingot |
| 1 gold concentrate | <ItemLink id="autarkia:electrolyzer" /> | 160 ticks, 256 FE/t | 1 gold ingot, copper nugget (10%) |

The <ItemLink id="autarkia:blast_smelter" /> also smelts it, like a furnace.
