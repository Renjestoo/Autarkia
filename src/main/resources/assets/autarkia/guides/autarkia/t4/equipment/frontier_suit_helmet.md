# Frontier Suit Helmet

<ItemImage id="autarkia:frontier_suit_helmet" scale="2" />

The Frontier Suit Helmet is the Tier 4 upgrade of the exo rig helmet. It keeps every exo rig helmet function and adds
night vision. It runs on FE.

## Get it

| Inputs | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:exo_rig_helmet" />, 5 <ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_steel_ingot" />, 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:advanced_circuit" />, 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:battery_bank" />, 1 golden carrot | <ItemLink id="autarkia:fabricator" /> | 400 ticks, 375 FE/t (150,000 FE) | 1 Frontier Suit Helmet |

The FE in the old exo rig helmet goes into the new helmet.

## Use it

1. Charge the helmet in the item slot of a <ItemLink id="autarkia:battery_bank" /> (2,048 FE/t) or a
   <ItemLink id="autarkia:capacitor_bank" /> (512 FE/t).
2. Wear it. While the suit is on and the helmet has FE, it works by itself.
3. Press the suit key (default N) to turn all worn suit pieces on or off. The action bar shows "Suit on" or "Suit off".

### Functions

- Night vision for 10 FE/t. The effect shows no particles. A night vision potion stays when you turn the suit off.
- +1 oxygen bonus: you stay longer under water.
- Every 20 ticks it shares the FE of all worn suit pieces, in proportion to their capacity.

## Numbers

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Armor | 3 |
| Armor toughness | 3 |
| Knockback resistance | 0.05 |
| Energy | 2,000,000 FE |
| Charge rate | Up to 5,000 FE/t |
| Night vision | 10 FE/t |
| Enchantability | 15 |
| Repair | Vantite steel ingot on an anvil |

The tooltip shows "stored / 2000000 FE" and "Suit: on" or "Suit: off".

## Problems

- No night vision: the helmet has no FE, or the suit is off. Charge it, or press the suit key.
- "No suit pieces worn": you pressed the suit key without any suit piece on.
