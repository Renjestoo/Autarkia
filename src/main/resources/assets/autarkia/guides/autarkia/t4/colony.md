# Colony and Outposts

The <ItemLink id="autarkia:core_upgrade_t4" /> raises your Colony Core to Tier 4. The domain grows to a half-size of
48 blocks and the worker cap to 20.

At Tier 4 the colony can have 6 outposts. An outpost is a small second base at a far ore deposit, 64 to 512 blocks
from the Core. An <ItemLink id="autarkia:outpost_seed" /> starts it: an outpost core grows from the seed. Each outpost
has its own square (half-size 24) inside the colony domain. Workers build a depot, an HV pylon, a wind tower, a
recharge dock and a jaw crusher there.

An HV link brings power between the outpost and the main base. A cable car line brings the goods. The colony plans
both by itself. While nobody is near an outpost, it keeps a share of its production on paper. Read
[Outpost Seed](colony/outpost_seed.md) for all rules.
