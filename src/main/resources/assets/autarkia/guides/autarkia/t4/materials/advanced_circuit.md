# Advanced Circuit

<ItemImage id="autarkia:advanced_circuit" scale="2" />

The advanced circuit is the control part of Tier 4. Every Tier 4 machine, the outposts, the Tier 4 workers and the
FE equipment need it.

## Get it

| Input | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 <ItemLink id="autarkia:basic_circuit" /> and 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_ingot" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:electrolyzer" /> | 400 ticks, 256 FE/t (102,400 FE) | 1 advanced circuit |

Put the basic circuits in one input slot and the vantite ingot in the other.
