# Raw Vantite

<ItemImage id="autarkia:raw_vantite" scale="2" />

Raw vantite is the ore you get from vantite ore. A furnace cannot smelt it. Process it in a Tier 2 or Tier 3 machine,
or crush it for more ingots.

## Get it

- Mine <ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_ore" /> with a diamond level pickaxe. Fortune gives more.
- Break a vantite <ItemLink id="autarkia:ore_outcrop" />: 1 raw vantite.
- Take 9 back out of a <ItemLink id="autarkia:raw_vantite_block" />:

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:raw_vantite" />

## Use it

| Input | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 raw vantite | <ItemLink id="autarkia:blast_smelter" /> | 200 ticks, 64 FE/t | 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_ingot" /> |
| 1 raw vantite | <ItemLink id="autarkia:jaw_crusher" /> | 100 ticks, 192 FE/t | 2 <ItemLink id="autarkia:crushed_vantite" /> |
| 1 raw vantite | <ItemLink id="autarkia:stamp_mill" /> | 160 ticks, 16 FE/t | 1 crushed vantite, plus 1 more (50%) |
| 1 raw vantite | <ItemLink id="autarkia:quern" /> | 8 turns by hand | 1 crushed vantite, plus 1 more (50%) |

The blast smelter must be hot before it works. Read its page.
