# Frontier Suit Leggings

<ItemImage id="autarkia:frontier_suit_legs" scale="2" />

The Frontier Suit Leggings are the Tier 4 upgrade of the exo rig leggings. They keep the step assist and add more
speed while you sprint. They run on FE.

## Get it

| Inputs | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:exo_rig_legs" />, 7 <ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_steel_ingot" />, 2 <ItemLink id="autarkia:servo" />, 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:advanced_circuit" />, 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:battery_bank" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:fabricator" /> | 450 ticks, 389 FE/t (175,050 FE) | 1 Frontier Suit Leggings |

The FE in the old exo rig leggings goes into the new leggings.

## Use it

1. Charge the leggings in the item slot of a <ItemLink id="autarkia:battery_bank" /> (2,048 FE/t) or a
   <ItemLink id="autarkia:capacitor_bank" /> (512 FE/t).
2. Wear them. While the suit is on and the leggings have FE, they work by themselves.
3. Press the suit key (default N) to turn all worn suit pieces on or off.

### Functions

- Step assist: you step up blocks of up to 1.1 blocks of height (+0.5 step height). It costs 2 FE/t while you move
  on the ground.
- Sprint boost: +20% speed while you sprint, for 10 FE/t.

## Problems

- No step assist and no sprint boost: the leggings have no FE, or the suit is off.
