# Vantite Blade

<ItemImage id="autarkia:vantite_blade" scale="2" />

The vantite blade is the sword of Tier 4. It needs no FE. Its use dashes you forward, and the next hit on a hostile
mob does more damage.

## Get it

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:vantite_blade" />

## Use it

1. Hold the blade and attack like with any sword.
2. Look in a direction and press use. You dash 6 blocks forward, in 3 ticks of 2 blocks. A wall stops the dash.
3. Hit a hostile mob within 10 ticks after the dash. The first hit does 4 more damage.
4. The dash costs 12 durability. Then the blade has a cooldown of 80 ticks.
5. Repair the blade with <ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_steel_ingot" /> on an anvil.

The dash resets your fall distance while it moves you.

## Numbers

| Property | Value |
|---|---|

| Dash bonus | +4 damage on the first hit on a hostile mob within 10 ticks |
| Cooldown | 80 ticks (4 s) |
