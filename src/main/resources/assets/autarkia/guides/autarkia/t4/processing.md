# Froth Flotation

Tier 4 adds one processing machine: the <ItemLink id="autarkia:froth_flotation_plant" />. It is the last step of the
4x ore line.

## The 4x ore line

1. The <ItemLink id="autarkia:jaw_crusher" /> crushes 1 raw ore into 2 crushed ore.
2. The froth flotation plant turns 1 crushed ore and 250 mB of water into 2 concentrate.
3. A furnace, a blast furnace, the <ItemLink id="autarkia:blast_smelter" /> or the
   <ItemLink id="autarkia:electrolyzer" /> turns 1 concentrate into 1 ingot.

This gives 4 ingots for each raw ore. Each flotation run also gives slag (50% chance) and a nugget of a second metal
(35% chance). Vantite concentrate needs the blast smelter or the electrolyzer. A furnace cannot smelt it.

Read [Vantite and Concentrates](materials.md) for all concentrates.
