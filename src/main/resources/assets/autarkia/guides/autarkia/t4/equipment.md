# Vantite Equipment

Tier 4 equipment uses vantite steel. Most pieces run on FE. An FE item holds its energy instead of durability. Charge
it in the item slot of a battery bank (2,048 FE/t) or a capacitor bank (512 FE/t). The items take up to 5,000 FE/t.

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| <ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_blade" /> | Sword, 8.5 damage. Use: dash 6 blocks forward. |
| <ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_excavator" /> | Pickaxe and shovel, netherite level. Modes 1x1, 3x3 and vein. |
| <ItemLink id="autarkia:grapple_launcher" /> | Fires a hook up to 48 blocks and pulls you to it. |
| <ItemLink id="autarkia:frontier_suit_helmet" /> | Night vision. |
| <ItemLink id="autarkia:frontier_suit_chest" /> | Wind and knockback resistance. |
| <ItemLink id="autarkia:frontier_suit_legs" /> | Step assist and sprint speed. |
| <ItemLink id="autarkia:frontier_suit_boots" /> | Jet boots and fall protection. |
| <ItemLink id="autarkia:rail_rifle" /> | Charged hitscan shot through up to 5 targets, 96 blocks. |

The fabricator makes all of them except the blade. The suit pieces and the rail rifle upgrade a Tier 3 item: the
exo rig pieces and the arc rifle. The energy in the old item goes into the new item.

The suit key (default N) turns all worn suit pieces on or off. The mode key (default V) changes the mode of the
excavator.
