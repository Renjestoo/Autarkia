# Vantite Equipment

The FE items of tier 4 charge at up to 5,000 FE/t. Without FE they mine at speed 1 and drop nothing. The fabricator
makes all of them except the blade.

## Tools and weapons

- <ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_excavator" />: pickaxe and shovel, netherite level, speed 16, 2,000,000 FE. The mode
  key (V) cycles 1x1, 3x3 and vein. 250 FE per block, 3x3 at half speed. Vein mode breaks up to 64 connected blocks of
  the same ore (stone and deepslate ore count as one vein) at half speed, for 400 FE per block.
- <ItemLink id="autarkia:grapple_launcher" />: 1,000,000 FE. A shot costs 2,000 FE. The hook flies 3 blocks per tick,
  range 48. Hold use to pull at 1.2 blocks per tick (20 FE/t). Sneak to hang in place (5 FE/t). Release to let go. No
  fall damage for 40 ticks after you let go. Cooldown 10 ticks.
- <ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_blade" />: sword, attack 8.5, durability 3,000. Use: dash 6 blocks forward for 12
  durability, cooldown 80 ticks. The first hostile mob you hit in the dash takes 4 more damage. Repair it with vantite
  steel on an anvil.
- <ItemLink id="autarkia:rail_rifle" />: 3,000,000 FE, 20,000 FE per shot. Hold use 10 to 30 ticks: damage 8 to 24.
  Hitscan 96 blocks, it goes through up to 5 entities and stops at blocks without damage to them. Knockback 1.5,
  cooldown 40 ticks.

## Frontier Suit

Each piece holds 2,000,000 FE and keeps every Exo Rig function. Defense 3, 8, 6 and 3, toughness 3, knockback
resistance 0.05 per piece.

- <ItemLink id="autarkia:frontier_suit_helmet" />: night vision, 10 FE/t.
- <ItemLink id="autarkia:frontier_suit_chest" />: +0.3 knockback resistance and +0.5 explosion knockback resistance,
  2 FE/t.
- <ItemLink id="autarkia:frontier_suit_legs" />: step assist to 1.1 blocks (2 FE/t) and +20% sprint speed (10 FE/t
  while you sprint).
- <ItemLink id="autarkia:frontier_suit_boots" />: jet boots. Hold jump in the air for +0.10 upward speed per tick, up
  to 0.5 blocks per tick, 80 FE/t. Fall damage is cancelled for 50 FE per block above 3.

The suit key (N) turns all suit functions on or off.
