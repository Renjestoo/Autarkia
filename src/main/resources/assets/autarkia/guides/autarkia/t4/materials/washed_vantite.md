# Washed Vantite Ore

<ItemImage id="autarkia:washed_vantite" scale="2" />

Washed vantite ore is crushed vantite without the rock. 3 washed ore give 4 ingots in the blast smelter.

## Get it

| Input | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:crushed_vantite" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:sluice" /> | 100 ticks, needs water at its head | 1 washed vantite, gold nugget (10%) |

## Use it

| Input | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 washed vantite | <ItemLink id="autarkia:blast_smelter" /> | 200 ticks, 64 FE/t | 4 <ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_ingot" />, <ItemLink id="autarkia:slag" /> (30%) |
| 2 washed vantite | <ItemLink id="autarkia:magnetic_separator" /> | 120 ticks, 128 FE/t | 3 <ItemLink id="autarkia:pure_vantite" />, 1 slag, gold nugget (50%) |

## Next

- Separate it to <ItemLink id="autarkia:pure_vantite" /> for 3 ingots from 2 washed ore.
- The <ItemLink id="autarkia:froth_flotation_plant" /> gives more: 4 ingots for each raw vantite.
