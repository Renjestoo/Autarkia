# Vantite Steel Ingot

<ItemImage id="autarkia:vantite_steel_ingot" scale="2" />

Vantite steel is an alloy of vantite and steel. It is the structure metal of Tier 4: the HV line, the generators, the
flotation plant, the outposts and the vantite equipment need it.

## Get it

| Input | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_ingot" /> and 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_ingot" /> | <ItemLink id="autarkia:blast_smelter" /> | 300 ticks, 64 FE/t | 1 vantite steel ingot |

Put each ingredient in its own input slot of the blast smelter. You can also take 9 ingots out of a
<ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_steel_block" />:

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:vantite_steel_ingot" />
