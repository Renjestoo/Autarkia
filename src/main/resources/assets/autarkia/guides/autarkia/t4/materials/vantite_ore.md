# Vantite Ore

<ItemImage id="autarkia:vantite_ore" scale="2" />

Vantite ore is the deep ore of Tier 4. Vantite goes into most Tier 4 machines, the outposts and the vantite
equipment. It lies far below the surface and caves rarely show it.

## Get it

Vantite ore generates in stone and deepslate in all Overworld biomes. It never generates in the Nether or the End.

| Vein type | Y range | Vein size | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small vein | -64 to 0, most near Y -32 | 5 | 3 tries in each chunk |
| Deposit | -56 to -16 | 12 | 1 in 16 chunks |

A small vein leaves out half of its ore blocks that touch air, so most small veins are hidden in the rock. A deposit keeps
all its blocks. A deposit with 8 or more ore blocks puts an <ItemLink id="autarkia:ore_outcrop" /> of the vantite
kind on the surface above it. The outcrop appears only when the surface is at most 128 blocks above the deposit.

## Use it

1. Find a vantite outcrop on the surface. Jade shows "Marks a vantite deposit below".
2. Dig down below the outcrop to Y -16 or lower.
3. Mine the ore with a <ItemLink id="autarkia:steel_pickaxe" />, a diamond pickaxe or better.
4. You get 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:raw_vantite" />. Fortune gives more.
5. Silk Touch gives the ore block itself.

## Numbers

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Hardness | 5.0 |
| Blast resistance | 9.0 |
| Tool | Diamond level pickaxe (the steel pickaxe counts) |
| Drop | 1 raw vantite (Fortune adds more) |
| Silk Touch drop | Vantite Ore |
| Furnace | Does not smelt |

## Problems

- The ore breaks but drops nothing: your pickaxe is below diamond level. A bronze or iron pickaxe does not work.
