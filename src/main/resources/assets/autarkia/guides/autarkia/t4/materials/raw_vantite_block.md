# Block of Raw Vantite

<ItemImage id="autarkia:raw_vantite_block" scale="2" />

A storage block for 9 raw vantite. It saves space in chests and crates.

## Get it

<RecipeFor id="autarkia:raw_vantite_block" />

## Use it

1. Craft 9 <ItemLink id="autarkia:raw_vantite" /> into 1 block.
2. Put the block in a crafting grid to get the 9 raw vantite back.
3. Mine the placed block with a diamond level pickaxe. The steel pickaxe counts.
