# Cable Cars, Launchers and Depots

Tier 4 moves whole loads of items between far sites.

- The <ItemLink id="autarkia:depot" /> is the storage of a site. A depot near an outpost core belongs to that
  outpost. The ledger terminal shows the stock of every depot in its Sites tab.
- A <ItemLink id="autarkia:cable_car_station" /> at each end makes a cable car line, 16 to 160 blocks long. Gondolas
  carry 27 stacks each and run all the time.
- A <ItemLink id="autarkia:crate_launcher" /> throws a full crate on an arc into a
  <ItemLink id="autarkia:catch_net" /> 8 to 96 blocks away. The net puts the items into the storage below it.

A depot pushes ordered items into a cable car station or a crate launcher next to it. So a request in the Sites tab
travels by cable car or by crate to the main site. Strong wind swings the gondolas and pushes the crates, so both
check the wind.
