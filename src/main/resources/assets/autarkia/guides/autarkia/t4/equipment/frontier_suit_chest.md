# Frontier Suit Chestplate

<ItemImage id="autarkia:frontier_suit_chest" scale="2" />

The Frontier Suit Chestplate is the Tier 4 upgrade of the exo rig chestplate. It keeps the exo rig jump boost and
adds wind resistance: knockback and explosions push you less. It runs on FE.

## Get it

| Inputs | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:exo_rig_chest" />, 8 <ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_steel_ingot" />, 2 <ItemLink id="autarkia:advanced_circuit" />, 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:battery_bank" />, 2 phantom membranes | <ItemLink id="autarkia:fabricator" /> | 500 ticks, 400 FE/t (200,000 FE) | 1 Frontier Suit Chestplate |

The FE in the old exo rig chestplate goes into the new chestplate.

## Use it

1. Charge the chestplate in the item slot of a <ItemLink id="autarkia:battery_bank" /> (2,048 FE/t) or a
   <ItemLink id="autarkia:capacitor_bank" /> (512 FE/t).
2. Wear it. While the suit is on and the chestplate has FE, it works by itself.
3. Press the suit key (default N) to turn all worn suit pieces on or off.

### Functions

- Wind resistance: +0.3 knockback resistance and +0.5 explosion knockback resistance, for 2 FE/t.
- Jump boost: +0.25 jump strength, for 100 FE for each jump. It needs at least 100 FE.

## Problems

- The resistance and the jump boost stop: the chestplate has no FE, or the suit is off.
