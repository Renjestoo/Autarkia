# Vantite Concentrate

<ItemImage id="autarkia:vantite_concentrate" scale="2" />

Vantite concentrate comes from the froth flotation plant. 1 crushed vantite gives 2 concentrate, and each
concentrate gives 1 ingot. This is the best Tier 4 vantite line: 4 ingots for each raw vantite.

## Get it

| Input | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:crushed_vantite" /> and 250 mB water | <ItemLink id="autarkia:froth_flotation_plant" /> | 200 ticks, 768 FE/t | 2 vantite concentrate, <ItemLink id="autarkia:slag" /> (50%), gold nugget (35%) |

## Use it

| Input | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 vantite concentrate | <ItemLink id="autarkia:blast_smelter" /> | 200 ticks, 64 FE/t | 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_ingot" /> |
| 1 vantite concentrate | <ItemLink id="autarkia:electrolyzer" /> | 160 ticks, 256 FE/t | 1 vantite ingot, gold nugget (10%) |

A furnace and a blast furnace cannot smelt vantite concentrate.
