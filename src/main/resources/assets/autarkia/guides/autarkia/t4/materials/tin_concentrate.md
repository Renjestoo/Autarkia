# Tin Concentrate

<ItemImage id="autarkia:tin_concentrate" scale="2" />

Tin concentrate comes from the froth flotation plant. 1 crushed tin ore gives 2 concentrate, and each concentrate
gives 1 tin ingot. With the jaw crusher this is 4 tin ingots for each raw tin.

## Get it

| Input | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:crushed_tin" /> and 250 mB water | <ItemLink id="autarkia:froth_flotation_plant" /> | 200 ticks, 768 FE/t | 2 tin concentrate, <ItemLink id="autarkia:slag" /> (50%), iron nugget (35%) |

## Use it

| Input | Machine | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 tin concentrate | Furnace | 200 ticks | 1 <ItemLink id="autarkia:tin_ingot" /> |
| 1 tin concentrate | Blast furnace | 100 ticks | 1 tin ingot |
| 1 tin concentrate | <ItemLink id="autarkia:electrolyzer" /> | 160 ticks, 256 FE/t | 1 tin ingot, iron nugget (10%) |

The <ItemLink id="autarkia:blast_smelter" /> also smelts it, like a furnace.
