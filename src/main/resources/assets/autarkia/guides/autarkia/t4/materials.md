# Vantite and Concentrates

Vantite is the metal of Tier 4. Most Tier 4 machines, the outposts and the vantite equipment need it.

## Vantite

<ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_ore" /> lies deep, from Y -64 to Y 0. An <ItemLink id="autarkia:ore_outcrop" /> on
the surface marks a large deposit below it. The ore drops <ItemLink id="autarkia:raw_vantite" />. A furnace cannot
smelt vantite. Use the blast smelter, the electrolyzer or the flotation chain.

Vantite has the same ore forms as the other metals:

| Form | Machine that makes it |
|---|---|
| <ItemLink id="autarkia:crushed_vantite" /> | Quern, stamp mill or jaw crusher |
| <ItemLink id="autarkia:washed_vantite" /> | Sluice |
| <ItemLink id="autarkia:pure_vantite" /> | Magnetic separator |
| <ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_concentrate" /> | Froth flotation plant |
| <ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_ingot" /> | Blast smelter or electrolyzer |

The blast smelter alloys 1 vantite ingot and 1 steel ingot into 1
<ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_steel_ingot" />. The electrolyzer makes the
<ItemLink id="autarkia:advanced_circuit" /> from 2 basic circuits and 1 vantite ingot.

Store 9 of a kind in a <ItemLink id="autarkia:raw_vantite_block" />, a
<ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_block" /> or a <ItemLink id="autarkia:vantite_steel_block" />.

## Concentrates

The froth flotation plant turns 1 crushed ore into 2 concentrate. 1 concentrate smelts into 1 ingot. With the jaw
crusher before it, 1 raw ore gives 4 ingots.
